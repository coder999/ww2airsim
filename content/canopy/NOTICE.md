# Procedural canopy material

Original mathematical crown-height material generated by this project under
its AGPL-3.0-or-later license. No photography or third-party imagery is used.
It represents generic forest structure, not historical tree locations.

Rebuild: `npx tsx tools/terrain/bakeCanopy.ts` (on Ryzen via `remote-run`).
`material.bin.gz` is a gzip-compressed 1024-square RGBA8 array: RG encode
slopes in the range -2.5 to +2.5; B is crown reflectance variation; A is
local enclosure. The periodic tile covers approximately 1,680 ft. Runtime
attenuates the slope strength, distorts coordinates slightly, and generates
mipmaps. Colors are assigned by the shader, not embedded sRGB pixels.
