# G4M Betty handoff: an original model with landing gear (2026-09-29)

Mark's note: the G4M Betty most needed work. Its hull was flat and it had no
landing gear. It was the CC-BY Sketchfab download by Jec_Games. It is now an
original Blender model on the DP1 pattern (`tools/models/blender/g4m-betty.py`,
`kit.Model(name, skin=1024)`, baked atlas, sidecar), with round lofted hull
sections and retractable main gear. The run was unattended, in worktree
`/home/mark/projects/ww2airsim-worktrees/g4m-betty`, branch `worktree-g4m-betty`
(from `main` eb8a854). **Not merged, not pushed to `main`, not deployed.** Master
spec §15 holds the status (Model detail pass row).

## What shipped

- **`tools/models/blender/g4m-betty.py`**: cigar hull lofted from round (super-elliptic)
  sections, glazed nose, raised glasshouse, tapered wing with control surfaces,
  two nacelles with cowl gills and exhausts, twisted three-blade propellers, a tall
  single fin and tailplane, a tail stinger gun, a dorsal turret, two beam blisters,
  main gear (legs, covers, wheels) and a fixed tailwheel, hinomaru with white borders.
- **`tools/models/entries/g4m-betty.json`** is now a Blender entry like the Ki-21's
  (`"skin": true`, 1024 px webp). Budget unchanged.
- **The rig row** in `src/render/scene/airframeRigs.ts`: props `Prop1`, `Prop2` are now
  three-bladed (the cited G4M1 has three; the download's four were wrong), and the row
  gains `GearL` and `GearR` (retract aft, like the Ki-21) beside `Turret1` (axis +y,
  unchanged). Nothing under `src/sim` changed; no scenario changed; overall span is
  24.89 m (81.7 ft), unchanged.
- **The Jec_Games credit is gone.** Credits are generated from entries
  (`modelCreditsIndex.ts`), so the source change removed it from the in-app list;
  `ASSETS.md` lost the CC-BY row and gained an "authored for this project" row, and the
  note that described the download was reworded. The download candidate in
  `tools/models/cache/mitsubishi_g4m.glb` is gitignored and was left in place. Older
  plans and handoffs still name the author; they are dated history.
- **Tests**: `modelCredits.test.ts` (string without Jec_Games), `provenance.test.ts`
  (g4m-betty is now a Blender model), `aircraftDimensions.test.ts` (row now held to 1%,
  read date 2026-09-29), Hangar check 16 (g4m-betty added), `flat-luminance.json`
  (see the caveat below).

That is 24% of the byte budget, 39% of the triangle budget and 13% of the draw budget.
Two consecutive `npm run models:build -- g4m-betty` runs wrote byte-identical glbs
(sha256 `8f70d177cbb544dd5b4de4a2de649ab522ef6750918b8330d1247f3f9c6dd5f3`), on nexus.
Overall length is held to the cited 65.5 ft (19.97 m) within 1%: the first build
measured 20.21 m because the nose tip and tail-gun muzzle stand past the hull stations,
so the script's `L` is `LENGTH / 1.0125` (measured 2026-09-29).

Rig pivots (entry `keep`): props at x 3.364 m, y 0.037 m, z +/-4.480 m (0.18 of the
span); main-gear hinges at x 1.569 m, y -0.397 m, inside the nacelle bottoms; the
turret at x -0.789 m, y 1.223 m. The wheels' lowest point is 2.47 m (8.1 ft) below the
origin. No flying aircraft spec draws the Betty, so the "stands on `gear.heightM`" rig
test is vacuous for it; the height is an ESTIMATE.

## Sources: cited vs estimated

CITED (English Wikipedia "Mitsubishi G4M", Specifications, G4M1 Model 11, read 2026-09-29):
span 24.89 m, length 19.97 m, wing area 78.125 m2, two Kasei 11 radials, three-bladed
propellers, armament positions (1 nose, 2 waist, 1 top gun, 20 mm tail cannon).

ESTIMATE (labeled in the script header): everything else. That includes propeller diameter
(11.2 ft), taper, dihedral, airfoil thickness, all hull station widths and heights,
engine spacing, gear geometry (wheel diameter 3.6 ft, leg length), the tailwheel being
drawn fixed, every control-surface span, the IJN green and undersurface colors (the
kit's `ijaGreen` and `underside` roles, generic colors), and the hinomaru sizes and
positions. No G4M marking source was read, so tail codes and unit markings are omitted
rather than guessed. No new entries in `tools/models/skin/colors.ts`.

## Verified vs not

Verified (2026-09-29):

- Vitest on nexus, only touched or related files: `tests/tools/models`,
  `tests/render/modelCredits.test.ts`, `tests/render/hangar`, `tests/render/roster.test.ts`,
  `tests/render/scene` (60 files, 920 passed, 7 skipped). That includes the rig test
  (prop 3-fold symmetry, gear retracts up and aft, no leg through the skin, turret axis),
  the skins test with the flat-shaded allowlist still at 9, and the entry, manifest and
  budget tests.
- Hangar Tier 2 on the reference GPU: the whole `hangar.spec.ts`, 18 of 18 on the final
  glb, including check 5 (lit/unlit), 8 (wireframe), 10 (budget as drawn, 38,936 tris,
  6 draws, not over), 15 (luminance) and 16 (checker luminance 0.0913 to 0.2523, 97.0%
  of pixels changed, restored exactly). An earlier full run failed check 1 once with
  "Browser closed" (a shared-server hiccup) and passed on the rerun.
- Determinism: two builds, identical bytes.

Not verified:

- **Check 15 has no true flat predecessor for the Betty.** The download measured 0.2838
  (a light livery, sha256 `639d204c...de82cd`), so it is not comparable (ratio 0.32),
  and a Blender entry cannot set `skin: false` to get a flat build of the new model.
  The fixture value 0.09133 is the new skinned build's own luminance, so check 15 is a
  regression guard for it, not a skin-versus-flat comparison. It agrees with the Ki-21's
  flat baseline (0.0908, same green, same lighting) within 1%.
- The in-game look. The Betty is not drawn by any scenario, so it was seen only in the
  Hangar and in Cycles.
- The gear's fold under the rig is proven by the rig test's geometry and by the gear-up
  capture, not by an in-flight retraction.
- The full suite and `npm run verify` are recorded in the ledger
  (`.superpowers/sdd/g4m-betty/progress.md`, gitignored).
