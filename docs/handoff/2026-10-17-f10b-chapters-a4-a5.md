## Design rulings taken (Mark may overrule)

1. **Boarding state lives in the `pickup` objective's own progress** (`count` = survivors aboard), not on the raft entity. It is mission state, so the world checksum, save and Continue already carry it, and `loop.ts` did not change. The raft's `survivors` is a static capacity. Proved: clone, JSON round trip, identical checksum, latched script.
2. **`landedNear` is measured at the touchdown** (the landing report has no position; read from the tracker), and any water landing while it waits is an **intermediate** landing (radio line, no debrief), hit or miss, so a miss can take off and retry.
3. **A4's F3 "stop below 1 kt, engines idle 10 s" at the anchorage is not built.** The shell raises the stop card and pauses at the landing report (rest below 1 m/s, 1.9 kt), so no dwell can follow it, and none can precede it. The final objective is `land` with `suggestedRecovery`: any friendly base (F5C D3), so Tacloban's runway also counts for the amphibian. Needs a ruling if the dwell matters.
4. **A4's launch**: the scenario carries its own `launch-1`; the site's stationary launch stays moored. Dressing only, no boarding role. Dye marker not drawn: a lamp stands in (night).
5. **A5's rally gate is a 9 km circle centred on the convoy, the protect window closes at 480 s** (`until: {at: 480}`), not "at the rally gate". The AI cannot be steered after a torpedo run (it holds its heading, or orbits the route end if its target died), so a distant gate is unreachable by design.
6. **A5 sites exclude Ormoc**: its pier AAA killed Avengers that held straight after their run; the fight is 11 km from the pier.
7. **A5 aircraft list is the F6F only; A4's is the PBY-5A only** (water-only eligibility).

## Findings

- **Closed: the A5 recovery finding.** F8C's 447 s (convoy off the pier) is now **380 s** at the formation's 85 m/s: Ormoc Bay lies mostly south-east of the pier, so the convoy sits nearer Bayug. `expectFail` removed; `tests/sim/missionFit.test.ts` flipped. First contact is 189 s, nearer than F3's "5-6 min" (declared).
- **The buoy cell is land to the sim** (height 0.15 m; water is a height at or below 0): the Catalina crashed at tick 31 from the F8C start. Moved 60 m seaward.
- **A force-3 sea costs a Catalina about 55 percent of its structure on a water takeoff** (wind 4.4 m/s; `water-quals` has that wind). 2.5 m/s costs none. Not changed in `water-quals`.
- **A scene `course` order cannot start a vehicle placed at speed 0** (`stepVehicle`'s at-rest shortcut). `launch-1` is placed creeping at 0.01 m/s. The fix is in `cues.ts`/`orderEntity` (F9) if wanted.
- **AI limits seen while tuning A5**: a homed leader goes RTB after 30 s without contact (so no `home`); a destroyer on the Avengers' approach axis shoots down three of four; after its run an Avenger holds heading forever (or orbits if its target sank); a torpedo-attack formation drops only the leader's torpedo (each Avenger needs its own ingress).
- **Unescorted, A5 behaves like the Escort mission**: easy loses 1+, normal and hard lose 2+ and the escort objective fails; escorted (fighters shot down 20 s after they appear, player pinned on the Avengers) none or one is lost at every difficulty.
- Water landing needs an airspeed under 42.7 m/s; touchdown rolls out about 400 m.
