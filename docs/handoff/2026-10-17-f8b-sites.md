## Deviations and rulings (check these)

1. **Bayug's runway is `content/bases/bayug.json` verbatim** (F8A precedent; Convoy Strike lands there), so Bayug sits 2.7 km west of its true position (a half
   mile east of Burauen). Its site adds a 500 x 900 m clearing the base lacks: airfield buildings are not kept clear of trees (only the strip and a clearing are), and
   the first captures showed the camp inside the forest. The runway-pin test now covers Bayug's runway and apron, plus "every F8B building stands inside its clearing".
2. **San Pablo `serviceable`: endgame true, alternate false** (plan ruling 3). A6's graph keeps three fields above a threshold and a secondary of "all three remain
   serviceable", which a never-serviceable field cannot satisfy; J6 asks only that San Pablo stay Japanese-controlled. One boolean and one test row each. **Mark may overrule.**
3. **The airfield schema needs one building**, so San Pablo before the invasion holds exactly one grown-over revetment (labeled), and the "built window has a building"
   test treats that window as declared-unoccupied, requiring it to hold nothing else.
4. **J3 is an F3 contradiction, not mine to settle.** J3 (3 Nov) recovers a Japanese pilot at Bayug; F3 section 5 puts 3 Nov in the "late Oct-Nov" column, where Bayug
   is American. The table keeps the literal date reading. The mission-fit row is a reported finding (below). Options: play the Burauen fields' J3 in `pre-invasion`, or change F3.
5. Plan said "reuse Tasks 1-9 unchanged"; I extended `expandScenario` (an optional `omit`) and the damage drawing in `airfield.ts` (site buildings only). Both are
   covered; nothing in sim collision, `serviceable` or the vehicle cap moved.
6. The first capture run's output was inside `test-results/`, which Playwright empties on each run; I lost 29 images once and re-shot. Captures now go to
   `E2E_CAPTURE_DIR` outside it. Editing source while a capture ran made one view time out (a dev-server reload); re-run alone.
