## 2. Rulings R1-R13 (the plan's; evidence in the plan's "Rulings" section)

- **R1.** The §3.1 instruments read the frozen `pursuit-tail-chase` fixture, because the shipped `pursuit-range` is now a green head-on merge.
- **R2.** The "actually hits" test moves to green by an in-test skill override, not by editing the frozen fixture.
- **R3.** Pursue and Extend steer through `steerToward`, which hands over to the lift-vector controller beyond 60° off the nose. With the G limiter and no lift-vector steering, 12 of 16 runs dove into the sea.
- **R4.** Break keeps 7b's velocity-controller flight, and there is no collision avoidance. Every avoidance variant cost the player's first-merge kill (7/8 to 0/8 at 97-114 m).
- **R5.** The negative-g floor is symmetric at -0.9 x `gLimit`, and 0 g with `negativeGCutout`. A -1 g floor cost the merge kill (3/8, 5/8, 2/8).
- **R6.** The ground floor acts below 400 m, or within 4 s of reaching it, and asserts never below 300 m. At the spec's 8 s, 0 of 4 loadouts got behind on the 7d bar; at 4 s, 4 of 4.
- **R7.** An overspeed guard was added to §3.2. Every full-throttle probe dive lost structure without it (F6F 0.834, Zero 0.000); with it, all kept 1.000.
- **R8.** The rejoin and the pursuit fly at full power. Both are needed: 5 of 16 re-engaged with the rejoin alone, 16 of 16 with both.
- **R9.** `zeroMerge.test.ts`'s kill floor drops from 4 to 1. The old 8/8 relied on the AI Zero cutting its own engine for 176 ticks.
- **R10.** The envelope-gating test uses the real A6M2 (wing-loading ratio 1.5958), not a synthetic Hellcat.
- **R11.** Safety overrides never change `decision.maneuver`. They record `decision.safety` and clear any latch.
- **R12.** Lag pursuit latches (entry at closure 40 m/s, exit at 15 m/s). The split-S and the scissors also require the threat behind the 3/9 line.
- **R13.** The re-engage test allows 150 s. The slowest measured run is 118.9 s.

The controller's execution rulings are in section 7, each with its cost if wrong.
