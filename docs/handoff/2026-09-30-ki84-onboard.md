## Decisions Mark should look at

- **Gear.** Retractable mains and tailwheel (the report says both; the model has all three rig nodes). `heightM` 2.093, `mainX` 0.100,
  `thirdX` -5.015, `thirdHeightM` 0.586, measured from the mesh. `travelSeconds` 4 is one pilot's estimate, ESTIMATE-grade.
- **The stance is 16.4 degrees tail-down** (the Zero and Ki-43 are about 12.3): long drawn legs. The consequence is conformance test 6 failing
  at 0.884 of target: the ground pitch ceiling equals the rest pitch (16.42 degrees), so a firm pull unsticks at 44.36 m/s, 1.00 x the
  model's own clean stall of 44.22 m/s. The floor was widened to 0.85 for this id with a comment, as the B-17's was; the fix would be
  shorter drawn legs. Verified by computing rest pitch, ceiling and stall through tsx.
- **Stores.** Two 1,000 lb `an-m65`, against a historic 2 x 30 kg or 2 x 100 kg (Wikipedia alone: 2 x 250 kg): 4.5 times the primary maximum
  per rack. Labeled Dev stores. The alternative is guns only.
- **The 0.68 propulsive efficiency was chosen using the climb** as a tie-break between two speed-equivalent pairs (0.75 reads climb +27.6%). Disclosed
  in the spec.
- **Power.** Military rating, not take-off power, at sea level (1,765 hp, from one low-confidence scan read on sheet 156A-2), and no
  water-methanol boost (about 2,050 hp WEP, named in the spec). The three highest curve points are FITTED, so their card rows prove nothing.
- **Span** 11.238 m (Wikipedia and the model) against 11.308 m (T-2 and 156A), 0.62% narrower than the primary source. Guns: hit points are
  the Zero's; armor and self-sealing tanks are not represented.
- **Cosmetic.** The Form 3 line reads "Top speed 426 mph at 22,999 ft" (7,010 m converts to 22,999.3 ft); the sourced figure is 23,000 ft.
  Not changed, since the reference altitude also anchors a fitted curve point.
- **Roll, dive limit (216 m/s), g limit (7.5), control rates, flap travel, tailwheel steering are ESTIMATES** (Zero and F6F values carried over).
- **Flap `clIncrement` 0.399 is circular**, derived from the two stalls. The Fowler-type flap is drawn and modeled as an ordinary flap.
- **Not in mission scenarios or AI rosters** (D14); Japanese, so the sortie picker offers it in Dev only.
