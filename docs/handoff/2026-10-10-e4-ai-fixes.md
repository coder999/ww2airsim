## The Escort regression, found after the merge, and its fix

The coordinator merged this branch with `main` after the Escort mission landed and `escort.test.ts`'s "unescorted at easy/normal: the Zeros shoot down more than one bomber" failed: **0 bombers lost in 600 s**.

- **Cause, bisected** with one change switched off at a time (the Escort scenario, unescorted, ryzen): bombers lost at easy / normal were 0 / 0 with every E4 change; **2 / 3 without lift-vector pursuit**; 0 / 2 without the angle-of-attack limit; 0 / 0 without the stall-speed unload, or without the rudder limit. The lift-vector law, flown at any target, dove the veteran `zero-1` from 800 m above under the level bombers to 70 m/s and 2,420 m, with no hits in its first pass (on `main` it landed 75).
- **Fix:** the veteran flies the lift vector only while the target is turning (`pursuitSteering`, `LIFT_PURSUIT_TURN_RATE_RAD_PER_S` = 0.05 rad/s, the 7c selector's own turning reading). The 70° bank only limits a pursuit of a target that turns; against a straight one, 7c's steering stands.
- **After:** the Escort scenario unescorted loses **2 / 4 / 4** B-17s at easy / normal / hard (over terrain, ryzen); `escort.test.ts` passes at all three.
- **So it cannot regress silently:** `tests/sim/ai/bomberAttack.test.ts` flies the Escort interceptors on the bombers without terrain for 300 s at Normal. It asserts `zero-1` lands at least 100 hits and at least two B-17s are down. Measured: 200 hits and two down with the fix; 0 hits with the lift vector at any target, where the test fails (run both ways, 2026-10-10).

## Wingman commands

| Key | Call | What the wingman does | Radio (text-only) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `2` | Cover me | Flies trail cover (7f's `TRAIL_COVER`) and fights only what attacks the player or itself. | Wingman: Roger, I'm on your tail. |
