## Wingman commands

| Key | Call | What the wingman does | Radio (text-only) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `1` | Attack | Fights the autopilot's target while Shift holds one, else the nearest enemy within 60° of the player's nose. The call lapses when that target is down. With none ahead, it is cleared to engage anything. | Wingman: Roger, engaging. |
| `2` | Cover me | Flies trail cover (7f's `TRAIL_COVER`) and fights only what attacks the player or itself. | Wingman: Roger, I'm on your tail. |
| `3` | Form up | Keeps its finger-four slot, even while the player fights; fights only in self-defense. | Wingman: Roger, rejoining. |

- Every live AI flying on the player obeys; with none, the key does nothing and nothing is said. Absent a call, a wingman flies 7f's rule exactly as before.
- The digit keys were free in flight: only the replay's own key map binds them, and it owns the keyboard while a replay is up. Legend rows added. Quick taps are latched in `main.ts` like the other edge-triggered keys.
