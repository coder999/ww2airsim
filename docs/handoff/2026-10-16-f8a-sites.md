## Deviations from the plan (and why)

1. **No `sites` step inside `worldFromScenario`.** The expansion runs in the two bundle loaders (`bundleForScenario`, `loadScenarioBundle`)
   instead, before the spec tables are gathered, because the vehicles and parked aircraft a window adds need their specs loaded. A scenario
   with no `sites` is returned as the very same object (a test asserts identity), so the "byte-identical" rule holds trivially; the golden recordings pass in the verify run.
2. **Two chapter-to-window readings, recorded in `worldStates.ts` and `docs/campaign-data.md`:** A1 plays in `pre-invasion` (it launches at
   0800, before H-hour; its success is the capture), and J4 (3 Dec) plays in `buildup`. Also: the table gives the window a chapter is *played* in
   and what its *success writes*; nothing yet makes a launch read `Campaign.world` (F8C's round trip). Question for Mark: should a launch
   read the stored window instead of the chapter's own?
3. **Tanauan has no field in `pre-invasion` and `assault`** (`built: false`: the airfield is absent from the world), not an empty field, and
   its base building list is empty (everything is `added` per window). It is `serviceable: false` in every window, asserted.
4. **Glbs through a `model` flag, not a global switch,** so "the three current bases render unchanged" is true and testable.
5. **Dulag's second "hangar" is a Quonset** (`dulag-shed-1`, labeled inferred): the base file's 12 x 16 m maintenance shed does not match
   any approved model, and a placement must be its glb at natural size.
6. **Plan line reference:** the plan cites `src/campaign/schema.ts:104` for `world`; it is line 123 now. No other named file or function was missing.
7. **Added beyond the plan:** a parked-aircraft cap of 8 per site-state (each is a stepped entity), `Building.heightM/headingDeg/state/model`,
   `Airfield.serviceable/clutter`, `Scenario.sites`. No recorder or save-schema change was needed: a record's content hash covers the expanded
   bundle, so a replay without the same `?siteState=` is refused as a content mismatch.
8. **`stepVehicle` change (Task 5):** skipping the re-pose of an at-rest vehicle saves 4 microseconds a tick for 12 vehicles
   (5.0 to 1.1 microseconds, `tools/sites/vehicleCost.ts`, three runs each). It rests on `vehicleOnTerrain` re-seating vehicles when terrain arrives; a test covers it.
