  history that disclose the estimates, the TAIC source). Every figure in the spec `source` carries SOURCED, DERIVED, FITTED or ESTIMATE
  and the conflicts name both figures.
- A `CARDS` entry, `MODEL_STANCE`, eye point, cockpit family (`japanese-bomber`, no sight), audio family (`multi_heavy`), `role: "transport"` (new schema value; it scores in the `transport` row ships already use), and a `bayDoors` block (ESTIMATE) so the two drawn cargo doors operate. - **The model's origin moved 1.025 m forward** (`normalize.origin` in `tools/models/entries/ki-57-ii-topsy.json`, glb rebuilt with
  `npm run models:build -- ki-57-ii-topsy`). The F4 build drew the wing quarter-chord at x 1.0 to 1.3 m, which `centerPoint.test.ts` rejects
  (the B-17 lesson in `docs/aircraft.md`). The wing is swept, so the quarter-chord runs 1.305 m to 0.745 m across the span; the 50%
  station (added to `STATION`) reads 0. Pivots in a manifest entry are in the SOURCE frame and stay as they were: shifting them with the
  origin turned the gear 1 m out of place (found by `aircraftRigs.test.ts`, 2026-10-14). Geometry, rig and node names are otherwise
  unchanged; the glb is 345,068 bytes (was 347,892).

## Conflicts reported, not resolved

- Top speed at 19,000 ft: TAIC 278 mph against 290 to 292 mph (Wikipedia, a kit page). The TAIC digit reads "2/8" in the scan and is fixed
  by the printed 241 kt.
- Gross 18,300 lb (TAIC) against 18,018 lb; empty 11,905 against 12,313 lb; ceiling 31,600 against 26,000 ft; 1,065 against 1,080 hp.
- The sheet's 1,430 ft take-off has no stated conditions (ground roll or over 50 ft), so it is not graded; the model's 366 m is 16%
  under the 436 m if it is a ground roll.
- Nothing documents the real tailwheel retracting; the model rigs it to, so it does.
