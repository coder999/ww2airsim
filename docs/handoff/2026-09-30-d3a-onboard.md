## Decisions Mark should look at

- **Fixed gear** required a sim change (D5). The alternative was a retractable stand-in, which would have been false.
- **Stores.** One 1,000 lb `an-m65` on a belly rack stands in for the historic 370 kg (250 kg centerline + 2 x 60 kg): +23% against the total,
  +81% against the centerline bomb. Labeled Dev stores. The alternative, guns only, leaves the bomb-drop path untested on the type.
- **Carrier true** (D3): passes the take-off matrix on every deck and the trap test. Trials on Akagi and Kaga are historic.
- **Take-off reads 56% long.** Cause: the power curve is the 0.84 MILITARY-rating fraction at sea level, so sea-level take-off runs 16% under the
  take-off rating. Reported with a 75% band, not tuned.
- **Source conflicts named in the spec:** Wikipedia's 267 mph against the TAIC speed sheet (spec picks TAIC), climb, fuel, length
  (10.79 m against 10.18 m), span.
- **Stalls, roll, dive limit, g limit, flap increment are ESTIMATE or circular.** A green card says the fit works, not that the airplane is right.
- **The drawn variant is unverified** (Model 11 or 22); the spec uses Model 22 figures.
- **Not modeled:** the rear gunner's gun, the two 60 kg wing bombs, dive brakes, engine-out, a cockpit model. Hit zones are estimates.
- **Not in mission scenarios or AI rosters** (D14); Japanese, so the sortie picker offers it in Dev only.
