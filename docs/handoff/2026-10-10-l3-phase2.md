# L3 Phase 2 prototype: invented 3D villages (2026-10-10)

Plan: [`superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-l3-land-quality.md`](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-l3-land-quality.md). Branch `l3-land-quality`, merged to `main` 2026-10-10 and removed.

## What changed since the first prototype

Mark disliked the painted houses, streets and roads: they were flat decals. They are removed.

- `tools/drape/gen.py` (`GEN_V2=1`) no longer paints houses, street grids or lanes. It keeps a faint bare-earth yard tone and the real OSM Maharlika Highway, muted. It writes 54 invented village seeds to `content/scenery/villages.json`.
- `src/render/scene/villages.ts` derives each village's layout from its seed, deterministically, and builds **3D gabled nipa-style huts** (woven walls, thatch, about 15% tin roofs) rather than `towns.ts`'s barrel-roof military huts. Huts are grouped per 2 km cell so frustum culling can drop whole cells.
- Trees are kept off every hut with the existing `nearTownHut` mechanism, using the square that bounds each rotated hut.
- Gated behind a DEV-only query, `?villages=on`. The shipping game is unchanged.
- Test: `tests/render/villages.test.ts` (5 tests: deterministic, plausible count, on land and out of airfield clearings, trees excluded at every corner, one mesh group per occupied cell). Passed on ryzen. `tsc` and eslint clean on ryzen.

## Measured (ryzen reference GPU, Mark's console session after a reboot, 1440p, clouds off, over the densest village)

Two runs. Treat every p95 as noisy: identical configurations swing by several ms between runs, and the GPU was shared with Mark's logged-in desktop.

**Run 1, one pass per view, GPU p50 / p95 in ms (variants run in this order; the last repeats shipping):**

| View | Shipping | `synth2` | `synth2` + villages | Shipping again |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| About 1,000 ft | 2.55 / 3.55 | 3.05 / 8.02 | 2.78 / 4.25 | 2.82 / 4.92 |
| About 3,000 ft | 3.08 / 8.31 | 2.59 / 7.99 | 2.62 / 3.08 | 2.56 / 4.67 |
| About 6,000 ft | 2.52 / 7.59 | 3.09 / 10.75 | 2.87 / 11.76 | 2.51 / 5.09 |

The same shipping configuration reads p95 7.6 and then 5.1 at 6,000 ft, and 8.3 then 4.7 at 3,000 ft. That spread is as large as any difference between variants, so run 1 cannot rank them.

**Run 2, 6,000 ft only, five interleaved repeats per variant (the view with the least headroom), medians:**

| Variant | GPU p50 | GPU p95 | Valid runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shipping (both batches) | 2.62 to 2.70 ms | 9.8 to 10.0 ms | 9 of 10 (one had only 85 GPU samples, under the spec's 100 floor) |
| `synth2` | 2.80 ms | 9.69 ms | 5 of 5 |
| `synth2` + villages | 2.98 ms | 10.56 ms | 5 of 5 |

**What this supports:**

- **The 6,000 ft view is over the 8.33 ms gate with nothing new in it.** Shipping terrain alone reads p95 of about 10 ms there on this run. That is a standing budget failure (`docs/testing.md` and `serverconfig/ryzen.md` record the budget specs failing on every run), so this branch cannot be blamed for it and cannot be cleared against it.
- **The new ground costs no measurable p50.** `synth2` is within run-to-run spread of shipping.
- **The villages may add roughly 0.2 to 0.3 ms at p50 at 6,000 ft** (2.98 against 2.62 to 2.70), the one place a signal shows. Individual p50 values overlap (villages 2.63 to 3.44, shipping 2.51 to 3.19), so this is suggestive and not proven.
- **Not measured:** the actual High tier with its own settings, and any view other than these three.

## Honest status

- The huts render and trees clear around them (screenshot below, `?villages=on` on the right). At 1,000 ft they read as small orange-brown boxes, **too saturated**. I did not tune the colors because I cannot verify a change without the GPU.
- Hut count was not recorded.
- The 13,000 ft budget view and the real towns (Tacloban, Basey) are not covered: `towns.ts` still draws its 24-hut Quonset rings there, and `villages.json` holds only invented seeds to avoid overlapping them.
