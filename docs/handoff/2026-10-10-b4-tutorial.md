## What was built

- **Basic Flying** (`content/scenarios/tutorial.json`, badge `basic-flying`), the **first mission in the picker** and preselected (the picker lists missions in `SCENARIO_OPTIONS` order). Parked at Tacloban's runway center, wind 3 m/s from 000, clear sky, 0900. Recommended loadout: bombs.
- **The new condition kind, `state`** (the "plane-state test", `CONTEXT.md`). As an objective (`kind: "state"`) it completes the first tick the living player's airplane matches every field given; as a trigger (`when: { state }`) it fires once. Fields, all optional, at least one required, validated by zod and `checkMission`:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `gear`, `flaps` | `up` (fully retracted) or `down` (fully extended); a gear in transit is neither |
| `bayDoors` | `shut` or `open`, same rule |
| `airspeedMps` | `[min, max]`, air-relative (wind subtracted, as the gauge) |
| `altitudeM` | `[min, max]` above sea level |
| `throttle` | `[min, max]` within 0 to 1, the lever |

  Code: `PlaneStateObject` in `src/sim/mission/schema.ts`, `planeStateHolds` in `step.ts`. `MissionTick` gained `wind` (one line in `loop.ts`). Nothing else in the sim changed; existing golden and determinism tests are untouched.
- **Ten chained objectives**, each with an "Instructor:" radio line when the step before it completes: full throttle, take off, gear up, climb above 1,500 ft, fly to the marker over the bay (the B1 arrow), strafe the two parked Hellcats, bomb the cargo ship (**secondary**), join the final (3 mi south), gear and flaps down under 120 mph, land.

## How each step is verified (`tests/sim/mission/missions/tutorial.test.ts`, 28 tests; `planeState.test.ts`, 24 tests)

- Flown for real through production `advance`: the throttle lever, the take-off roll, the gear coming up, the climb through the band, the final approach and landing (`fieldApproach`). Staged with `hold`: the legs to the marker and onto the final. Injected with `destroyNow`: the strafing and bomb kills (their own suites cover gunnery and bombing).
- The whole sortie ends in `missionOutcome` = success with the badge, no reasons, the nine messages in order, the bomb run left incomplete without costing the badge.
- Each step has a wrong-behavior test that keeps it incomplete: throttle at 0 / 0.5 / 0.89; sitting on the runway; gear lever left down, and gear still in transit; below and above the altitude band; marker missed sideways and crossed too low; one of two targets; final to the side and too high; gear, flaps and speed missing one at a time, in transit, and 119 versus 121 mph (air-relative, shown with the 3 m/s headwind).
- The tests can fail: with the altitude check removed and the speed limit loosened, 8 of 28 went red; with the gear and airspeed logic broken, 4 of 24 went red.
- The unit file also covers the schema (empty, inverted range, throttle outside 0..1, unknown key), a real B-17 opening its bay doors, a dead player, `after` gating, the trigger form and bit-identical determinism.

Full suite on ryzen, `npm run verify` (typecheck, lint, depcruise, tests): **395 files, 5,470 passed, 12 skipped, exit 0** (base 393 files, 5,411).

## Rulings I made

- **R1** Flaps are `up`/`down` only: the airplane has one flap setting, so "flaps 20 degrees" cannot be asked.
- **R2** **No Assist condition.** The Impact marker is render state (B2 R7); a sim condition cannot read it. The radio line teaches `U` and `V`, and the bomb run does not depend on it.
- **R3** The bomb run is secondary, so a player who picked the clean loadout can still finish.
- **R4** Text-only radio lines.
- **R5** Gunnery and the bomb run come before the landing (a landing ends the sortie).
- **R6** A state objective is instant, not held for N seconds.
- **R7** Picker row first among missions, name "Basic Flying".
- Pins I had to change for the extra row: the mission list in `content.test.ts` and `titleScreen.test.ts`. No behavior test changed.
