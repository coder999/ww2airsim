# DP2 handoff: ships (2026-09-28)

DP2 is the third plan of the model detail pass. Every ship now carries a baked
skin in its palette. The three Blender ships (Pennsylvania, Kagero, Casablanca)
were rebuilt to the downloads' level of geometry, and the four print-model
downloads (Essex, Cleveland, Mogami, Yamato), which have no usable UVs, are
box-projected. The design is
[`2026-09-28-model-detail-pass-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-model-detail-pass-design.md),
and the plan is
[`2026-09-28-dp2-ships.md`](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-28-dp2-ships.md).

The work is complete on branch `worktree-dp2-ships` and is **not merged**.
Merging into `main` is Mark's call. The branch forks `main` at `74c5b65`. No
scenario changed and nothing under `src/sim` changed. The `content/` files
that moved are the seven `content/ships/*.glb`.

## What shipped

- **The winding fix (`4ef028a`).** The kit's `tapered_box`, `cylinder` and
  turret parts wound inward. They now wind outward, and the three R2 ships were
  rebuilt (flat predecessors: Pennsylvania 548 triangles, Kagero 364,
  Casablanca 224). `WINDING_SHA` in the ledger holds those glbs.
- **The kit's ship parts.** `hull_lines` (sections, flare, sheer, waterline
  cut end caps, true-arc charts), `hull_at`, `naval_turret`. Read their
  docstrings in `kit.py`.
- **The text marking.** A stroke-font hull number in model space, refused
  mirrored or upside down (`tools/models/skin/strokeFont.ts`). No ship uses
  it: Task 1 found no visible hull number on any of the three cited hulls.
- **Ship skins.** `"skin": true` on a ship, palette paint (Ruling S1,
  `metallicFactor 0`), a pinned CC0 `deck-planks` scan sampled in world space
  on decks (Ruling S7).
- **Box projection** for the four downloads
  (`tools/models/skin/boxProject.ts`): a chart per role and dominant axis,
  shelf-packed at one texel size, seams split, never moved. `soupHash` proves
  the geometry is unchanged, and `FLAT_SOUP` pins the four flat hashes.

## Open

- **DP1** (first-generation aircraft: Ki-21, P-38, B-29 and the rest) and
  **DP3** are not started. DP1 and DP2 both edit `kit.py`, so DP1 is best run
  after DP2 merges.
- **Planks are invisible on the box-projected carrier.** The `deck-planks` scan
  has `tileM = 1.8`, and `sampleScan` picks a mip so the texel covers the
  footprint. At the Essex's 34.3 cm/px a tile is about 5 texels and the planks
  average to flat. Options: a bigger atlas, or a coarse per-deck plank tint or
  stripe layer. Not a defect in the pipeline.
- **The dark-green funnel cap.** The `dark` role is unmapped on some ships, so
  a funnel cap keeps the kit's dark green instead of the palette's.
- **The IJN linoleum decks** are drawn with the planks scan.
- **The stroke font's missing letters** (DP1 adds them).
- **Box-projected charts share texels** where a role overlaps itself. Harmless
  without markings.
- **Casablanca's dazzle** (Measure 32/15A) is not drawn: palette-only by
  Mark's ruling.
- **Texel and chart counts** for Cleveland, Mogami and Yamato were not
  recorded (the scratch script was lost); rerunning `boxProject` reproduces
  them.
- **`ships.spec.ts` sample counts.** Under shared desktop load the deck-quals
  and strike-range gates saw 13-29 GPU samples of 100 and failed the count
  guard; the flat baseline also gets about 15. It is the desktop's GPU sample
  flake, not the models (Ruling: not tracked further). `hangar.spec.ts` check
  1 timed out at 60 s under load and passed on rerun.

## Departures

The ledger's `Ruling:` lines, summarized:

- **S7:** decks sample the planks scan in world space (`world-xz`) instead of
  through each part's own UV parameterization (spec §4.2), so planks run
  continuously along a hull-length deck.
- **Task 10 plan defect:** the plan's fixtures used glTF-Transform
  `normals({overwrite:true})`, which un-indexes the mesh, so `boxProject`
  refused it; an indexed `smoothNormals()` helper replaced it.
- **Task 6:** `hull_lines` cuts each end cap along the waterline chord, because
  Blender's cap triangulation put triangles wholly below the waterline.
- **`smokeOrigin` moved** on the Pennsylvania (to its raked funnel's top) and
  on the Casablanca (to `[15, 22.2, 10.4]`), so `shipFitStage` still reaches
  the funnel; read each entry JSON for the value.
- **Placement:** parts stand on `stand(x, l)` (the lowest deck under them) and
  stacked tiers sink 0.02 m, instead of the plan's `deck(x)`.
- **Winding test:** Hull, MainDeck and Bottom are pooled into one signed
  volume, since each is an open shell alone.
- **Task 9:** 20 mm singles within 3 m of a twin 40 mm tub are skipped.
- **Convoy Strike and Task 13:** `spawnUrl` comes from `tests/e2e/harness.ts`;
  Shiratsuyu, not Fletcher, is the full-PBR reference.
- **The unskinned count** is 12 Blender entries, not the plan's 14.
