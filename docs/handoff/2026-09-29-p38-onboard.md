## Findings and misses (reported, not tuned)

- **ESTIMATE, no source:** roll rate, take-off distance, gear and flap travel times, lift-off speed, `gLimit`, steering numbers.
- **Stall reads 20% fast:** the shared `clMax` 1.4 is not this wing's (the sources imply about 2.0). Conformance test 6's upper
  bound was widened per model to 1.30 (measured unstick 59.3 m/s, 1.22 of target).
- **Climb above 20,000 ft is not right:** +15, +30, +75% at 25,000, 30,000, 35,000 ft; speed +3.5% and +6.3% at 30,000 and 35,000 ft. The
  single engine block and one power-fraction table cannot follow the turbo curve; the cards grade only up to 26,000 ft.
- **Gear discrepancy:** the schema needs tricycle `mainX` below zero (-0.01 modeled), the drawn mains stand at x +0.439. `gearContact.test.ts`
  carries a documented 0.45 m allowance for this model. The drawing is not edited (D4).
- **Stores:** 2 x 1,000 lb and 10 HVAR modeled against the same historic load (D7 to D9). Racks and rails sit outboard of the propeller
  discs (z 4.4 m and beyond) because the mounts slicer cannot read the inboard wing.
- **Engines:** two V-1710 as one block; both propellers turn; turbo power holds to altitude; engine-out is not modeled.
- **Guns:** hit points are gameplay values scaled like the Hellcat, not historic.
- No counter-rotating propellers, cockpit model, bay or dive-flap detail (deferred).
