# M2 handoff: AA fire (2026-10-10)

Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-10-m2-aa-fire.md`. Branch `m2-aa-fire` (worktree `~/projects/ww2airsim-m2`), cut from `main` `9070c285`. Not merged, not pushed. Run unattended; the viewing checkpoint is this document.

## What was built

- **One AA system for ships and ground batteries** (`src/sim/weapons/aaFire.ts`). Every number is in the one config object `AA_TUNING` (M5 scales it).
  - **Heavy AA** (turrets marked `aa: heavy` and `heavyAA`, plus one gun per ground battery): a fuse-timed shell. The director solves a constant-speed intercept (it leads a turning target by its present acceleration), adds aim and fuse-time error, and schedules a **flak burst** at the aimed point. When it goes off it damages every hostile airplane within 131 ft by `110 * (1 - d / r)` HP of structure, a blast like a bomb's. Range 1,640 ft to 3.4 mi (1.6 mi against a target under 1,000 ft).
  - **Light AA** (`lightAA`, plus a 40 mm twin per battery): tracer rounds through `flyProjectile`, led with E1's solve (extracted from `muzzleLeadDirection` as `solveMuzzleLead`, same arithmetic, so the AI is bit-identical), with aim error. Range by caliber: 20 mm 980 yd, 25 mm 1,200 yd, 40 mm 1,640 yd.
  - **Skill and ranging.** Each mount has its own skill (error scale 0.6 to 1.4). A gun that has just found you shoots wide (error x7, falling to x1 over 15 s light, 40 s heavy), starts over if you outrun its traverse (flying over it) or after a gap. That is what separates a lingering airplane from a fast pass.
  - **Both sides fire, every gun**, at any airborne (over 45 mph), unburnt airplane of the other side; never its own. Needs `sides` and `targetSides`; every old caller that passes neither has no AA.
  - **Credit.** The ship's or battery's id is the `attacker` and `lastHitBy` on the airplane it hit; they have no aircraft record, so nobody's `kills` rises and nothing friendly is recorded. Sinking, damage stages and fire are untouched. The debrief says "shot down by antiaircraft fire."
- **Cost bounds.** A ship-level range early-out, only airborne hostile airplanes, a cap of 600 live AA rounds and 192 pending bursts, AA rounds test only hostile airplanes (a 66 ft broad phase first), the sea and the ground, and record only hits on aircraft (no splash per round).
- **Audio.** `aa_gun` is a spatial loop per firing ship or battery (nearest three, within 1.6 mi, `LAYERS.aa_gun`); `flak_burst` plays at a burst within 1.9 mi, `flak_distant` beyond. Near flak also feeds the existing near-blast damage cue.
- **Render.** `flak.burst` fx recipe (flash and a black cloud, 4.5 to 7.5 s); `aaTracers.ts`, a separate thicker mesh (about 2 px at any range, 384 instances, nearest first) so airplane tracers are untouched.
- **Dev scenario `aa-range`**: 2,000 ft up, 2 mi from an anchored Fletcher flagged axis (so its AA is the calibrated one) with Tacloban's battery also hostile. **The Range Test's ships now shoot the player** (God mode makes it harmless).
- **A bug found on the way, fixed.** When the terrain arrives the browser rebuilt the structure list and dropped each structure's `side`, so every battery read as axis: Tacloban's own AAA fired at the Hellcat taking off from it, and friendly fire on structures was silently off in the browser. `rebuildStructures` keeps the side; `frame.test.ts` fails without it.

## Calibration (Moderate, measured)

Scripted Hellcat (the AI's velocity-following controller) over an anchored Fletcher in `aa-range`, only the destroyer shooting, 32 seeds each (exact and repeatable; `tests/sim/weapons/aaLethality.test.ts` pins them, `tools/ai/aaSweep.ts` re-runs with overrides):

| Profile | Result | Mark's target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circling 1,640 ft out at 300 ft, 195 kn | lost 32 of 32, **median 21.1 s** (13 to 36 s) | about 20 s |
| Straight pass over the ship at 300 ft, 290 kn | lost **2 of 32** (94% survive) | usually survives |
| Circling 6,600 ft out at 6,000 ft, 253 kn | lost 29 of 32, median 86.9 s | flak slower than the low guns |

"Lost" is on fire or destroyed. Ruling: "lingering" is a 1,640 ft orbit; a wider one is outside the light guns and lasts longer. A 64-seed run at the same numbers gave the same shape (orbit median 21 s).

Cost (aaCombat.test.ts): the Range Test with 13 ships (11 armed) all hostile and the Hellcat circling in the middle for 60 s, guns never losing it: **0.27 ms per 60 Hz tick** (16.7 ms is real time), 598 live AA rounds at the peak (cap 600), 70 bursts pending. The test asserts 10 s of sim in under 10 s of wall clock.

## Rulings (defaults taken unattended)

1. A light mount fires one aggregate round per salvo (one hit scale per round, not per barrel).
2. Flak shells are not flown; the burst point is fixed at fire time.
3. Airborne means over 45 mph.
4. A battery is one heavy gun and one 40 mm twin.
5. The Fletcher in `aa-range` is flagged axis (US ensign, so it reads odd).
6. "Lingering" is a 1,640 ft orbit at 195 kn.
7. Heavy guns reach only 1.6 mi against targets under 1,000 ft.
8. Mounts do not train or elevate toward their target: that needs the sim to publish a per-mount aim and is left to M3.
9. `MASTER_PLAN.md`: only the M2 bullet was edited as instructed, so Track I's "unwired: `flak_burst`, `aa_gun`" lines (Today and I3) are stale and need fixing on merge.
