The raw download came back, and the coordinator verified that a rebuild reproduces the committed glb byte for byte. So the B-17's code-drawn plates are gone, and its doors are real panels cut from its belly.

- **Branch history:** merged `origin/c1-surfaces-batch2` (`f152dc7c`: the split stage's `cut` and vector `pivot.axis`) into this branch. One conflict, in `aircraftRigs.test.ts`'s pinned lists, kept both sides.
- **The cut:** `BayDoor1L` and `BayDoor1R` are `split`s over the bay (output x -1.5 to 1.5, |z| up to 0.4), each hinged along x at its outboard edge.
  - The axis is oriented so a positive turn opens the door, the keel edge swinging down and out (`docs/models.md` §5).
  - The `cut` plane is not a hinge plane here. It is tilted between the belly skin and the download's interior shell, which runs 7.5 to 9.5 cm above the skin along the whole bay (ray-cast, source frame). So only skin comes away.
  - Driven by the sim's door fraction exactly as the drawn plates are: 85° fully open.
- **Painted from the model's own texture.** Both panels carry the body's `DefaultWhite_texture.png` material, and the captures show the silver skin and panel lines, not flat white. So the cut stage's UV bug on the F6F flaps did not show here, and there is nothing to coordinate on it yet.
- **No dark interior added.** Open, the cut leaves no see-through hole: the download's own interior shell fills the opening (black from below in the captures, with no background through it).

## What works

On the B-29, B-17, G4M and Ki-21:

- **`O` works the doors.** Travel time per airframe: B-29 2 s, B-17 10 s, G4M and Ki-21 6 s.
- **`V` with the doors not fully open drops nothing** and shows **BAY DOORS CLOSED** for 2.5 s, in the autopilot badge's row.
- **Open doors cost drag:** B-29 1.0 m², B-17 0.45, G4M 0.36, Ki-21 0.3 (ESTIMATEs). Bay bombs keep their stores drag (R4).
- **The flight-data strip (`I`)** has a BAY row (SHUT, TRANSIT, OPEN) on these four only.
- **The control legend** lists "Bay doors O".
- **Sound:** a synthesized motor runs while the doors travel, and they lock with the hook's clunk at either end.
- **AI stub:** an ingress raider whose spec has doors opens them inside 5,000 m of its destination with it ahead, and shuts them otherwise.
- **Hangar bench:** a "Bay doors" row with a slider and Cycle. The door hinges show as pivot gizmos.

## A decision made here, not by Mark

The recorded line said the doors would reuse C1's Blender hinge machinery. They are **drawn in code instead** (`src/render/scene/bayDoors.ts`), for all four bombers:

- The B-17 is a download whose raw source is not on nexus.
- A rebuild re-simplifies its whole body (split runs before simplify).
- Code serves all four in one path and leaves C1 batch 2's manifest work alone.

The cost is flat plates on a curved belly, offset 3.5 cm outward, painted a flat per-airframe color over a dark well plate. Modeling them into the glbs remains possible later; the `ponytail:` note in `bayDoors.ts` says where.
