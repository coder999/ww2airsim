## What works

On the B-29, B-17, G4M and Ki-21:

- **`O` works the doors.** Travel time per airframe: B-29 2 s, B-17 10 s, G4M and Ki-21 6 s.
- **`V` with the doors not fully open drops nothing** and shows **BAY DOORS CLOSED** for 2.5 s, in the autopilot badge's row.
- **Open doors cost drag:** B-29 1.0 m², B-17 0.45, G4M 0.36, Ki-21 0.3 (ESTIMATEs). Bay bombs keep their stores drag (R4).
- **The flight-data strip (`I`)** has a BAY row (SHUT, TRANSIT, OPEN) on these four only.
- **The control legend** lists "Bay doors O".
- **Sound:** a synthesized motor runs while the doors travel, and they lock with the hook's clunk at either end.
- **AI stub:** an ingress raider whose spec has doors opens them inside 5,000 m of its destination with it ahead, and shuts them otherwise.
- **Hangar bench:** a "Bay doors" row with a slider and Cycle. The door hinges show as pivot gizmos.

## A decision made here, not by Mark

The recorded line said the doors would reuse C1's Blender hinge machinery. They are **drawn in code instead** (`src/render/scene/bayDoors.ts`), for all four bombers:

- The B-17 is a download whose raw source is not on nexus.
- A rebuild re-simplifies its whole body (split runs before simplify).
- Code serves all four in one path and leaves C1 batch 2's manifest work alone.

The cost is flat plates on a curved belly, offset 3.5 cm outward, painted a flat per-airframe color over a dark well plate. Modeling them into the glbs remains possible later; the `ponytail:` note in `bayDoors.ts` says where.

## Measured

- **Belly stance:** ray-cast off each committed glb at nine stations per bay.

  | Airframe | Keel y | Hinge y |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | B-29, both bays | -1.449 | -1.318 |
  | B-17 | -0.904 | -0.838 |
  | G4M | -1.262 | -1.164 |
  | Ki-21 | -1.003 | -0.880 |

  `aircraftRigs.test.ts` holds each within 3 cm. A vertex-based measure misread two of them: the fuselage rings are too sparse, and the B-29's ventral whip antenna sits inside its forward bay at x 2.94.
- **Hangar side view, shut vs open:** 93 px (B-17) to 207 px (G4M) change. The front preset sees none on the B-17, whose chin turret hides its belly line.

## Tests

- **Sim:**
  - `tests/sim/bayDoors.test.ts`: travel, hold, the no-doors airplane, the drag scale, the gate's predicate (NaN reads shut), open doors slower through the real step, and the AI stub through production `advance`. Seen red with the stub forced off.
  - `combat.test.ts`: the release gate on all four bombers, enrolled and pinned to the specs with `bayDoors`.
- **Frame:** `frame.test.ts`, the `O` lever reaching `Controls`, the notice and its expiry, a fighter never showing it.
- **Audio:** `cues.test.ts`, the motor across frames that ran no tick, the clunk at each end, and the synth loop seam. The seam check is a second difference, seen red with a 110.5 Hz fault. A first-difference version passed that fault, which is why it changed.
- **Rendering:**
  - `bayDoors.test.ts`: doors shut under the belly and open hanging down. Seen red with the swing reversed.
  - `aircraftRigs.test.ts`: rigs with bays equal specs with doors, and the belly stance.
  - `cockpitFeedback.test.ts`: the BAY row.
- **E2E (nexus 680M):**
  - `hangar.spec.ts`: 7b (Cycle opens every bomber's doors, visibly) and 13 (door gizmos); 20/20.

  - 12/12, then the four bombers twice more, 8/8.
