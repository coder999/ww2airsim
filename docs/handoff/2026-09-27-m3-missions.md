## 4. History: verified claims and sources

All three missions read "This is an official U.S. Navy web site" NHHC pages
or M. Hamlin Cannon's *Leyte: The Return to the Philippines* (OCMH, 1954,
CMH Pub 5-9-1) — both public-domain federal works. Morison was neither read
nor cited anywhere in M3. `history.navy.mil` and `history.army.mil` return
HTTP 403 to automated fetches (both `curl` and WebFetch); NHHC pages were
read with `curl -k` (a browser user agent, TLS cert issue), and Cannon was
read on its HyperWar transcription (`ibiblio.org`) of the same public-domain
text, citing the CMH PDF URL that search indexes.

### Deck Quals — Wolverine and Sable

| Claim | Source | Supporting text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Side-wheel excursion steamers converted to training carriers | DANFS "Wolverine II" and "Sable" | "a side-wheel excursion steamer built in 1913..."; "the steel-hulled sidewheel Great Lakes excursion steamer..." |
| Operated on Lake Michigan, far inland | NHHC "The Navy's Historic Aircraft Wrecks in Lake Michigan" | "The Navy thought the Lake Michigan area, because it was so far inland, was an ideal training ground..." |

| Claim | Source | Supporting text |
| --- | --- | --- |

**Date discrepancy, in the text (not resolved, both readings valid):**
**Cannon dates the destruction of this convoy to 10 November**, with 4
destroyers sunk at the mouth of Ormoc Bay. **DANFS Ticonderoga and NHHC
H-Gram 040-2 both date the strike to 11 November.** The shipped text follows
DANFS/H-Gram (11 Nov), because they are the more detailed, ship-specific
primary sources and agree with each other independently; Cannon's date
stands uncorroborated elsewhere in his own text. This is worth a source note
if it is ever revisited, not a bug.
