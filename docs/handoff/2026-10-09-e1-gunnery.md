**Merged to `main`** 2026-10-09 (Mark: "merge now and fly on main"), with the retune in Rulings below. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-e1-gunnery.md`. Run unattended. The checkpoint is this document, because nothing changes on screen except how AI aircraft shoot.

## What changed

**1. The firing solution is honest** (`src/sim/ai/pursuit.ts`, `muzzleLeadDirection`).
- The lead is on the **relative** velocity, because rounds inherit the shooter's velocity.
- Drag slows the inherited part too, and the solution allows for that.
- The aim rises by the **drop** over the time of flight.

One round fired along the solution, against a target flying straight:

| Geometry | Miss before | Miss after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tail, 330 yd | 1.9 ft | 0.4 in |
| Abeam wingman, same speed, 330 yd | 129 ft | 0.3 in |
| 30° deflection, 440 yd | 93 ft | 0.9 in |
| Head-on, 550 yd | 3.3 ft | 0.8 in |
| Climbing target, 380 yd | 33 ft | 0.5 in |

**2. Fine tracking** (`gunTrackingControls`).
- The problem: the velocity controller's pitch is proportional, with rate damping. Tracking a turning target, it stood 3.4-5° off the solution in a 2 g turn, outside the veteran's 1.8° gun cone, so the veteran almost never fired.
- The fix: within 12° of the solution, the pilot commands body rates instead. It feeds forward the solution's own swing and adds a correction proportional to the nose error.
- In the tail chase that took the veteran from 6 rounds to steady fire, within 0.2-1° of the solution.

**3. Per-skill aim** (`PilotSkill`, `src/sim/ai/pilot.ts`).

| Field | Veteran | Green | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `aimErrorRad` | 0.025 (was 0.002 before the retune) | 0.026 (was 0.008) | Standard deviation of aim error per axis, drawn at each rescore. Higher is worse. |
| `gunTracking` | 1 | 0 | How much fine tracking the pilot uses. 0 points the flight path as before. |

**4. Two safety fixes that honest kills exposed** (`src/sim/ai/safety.ts`).
- **Floor recovery after a kill.**
  - A low pursuer whose target dies is suddenly under the 1,300 ft floor. It used to pull at full G until vertical.
  - A green Zero then stalled at 25 mph and fell into the sea.
  - A recovery that is already climbing now climbs out at 20° instead.
- **Load-factor limit and control noise.** The limiter now also bounds the control noise. Green's noise, added after the limiter, put a defensive break at 7.54 g against a 7.5 g limit.

**5. Replay records every other tick** (`REPLAY_MIN_TICK_GAP` 1 to 2, `src/replay/recorder.ts`).
- The retuned AI keeps more rounds in flight. The busiest scenario's 10 s of replay grew from 52.6 MB to 66.8 MB, over spec §9's 64 MB.
- Every other tick is spec §9's own first step. It now reads 42.8 MB. Replay already blends between recorded worlds, and `instantReplay.spec.ts` passes 2 of 2 on nexus's GPU.
- The revert is one line, if you see a difference.

## Rulings (Mark, 2026-10-09, after reading the first draft)

1. **Tone veterans down now**: a straight-flying player at 550 yd should survive a veteran for a median of about 8 s, not about 3 s, with veteran-on-green duels still ending in kills most of the time. Done: `VETERAN_SKILL.aimErrorRad` 0.002 to 0.025 (1.4°). Slowing the fine tracking (`gunTracking` 0.5-0.7) instead barely slowed the passive kill (3.8 s) and wrecked the duels (0-3 of 8 tail kills on a green), so it was not used.
2. **Make green hit harder**, keeping the 7d bar in all its runs. Done, within that limit: `GREEN_SKILL.aimErrorRad` 0.008 to 0.026. The tradeoff:
   - Any fine tracking above 0.1 broke the 7d bar (0.2: 8 of 16 runs; 0.3: 5 of 16; 0.6 and 1: 1 of 16). So green still does not fine-track, and the gain is small.
   - Widening green's gun cone (`gunneryAccuracy` 1.3-2.0) gave 1-4 of 24 green-v-green kills, no better, so the cone stays at 1.0.
   - Aim errors from 0.028 to 0.04 sent one cursor of `aiReengage`'s green Zero off extending for 130 s.
3. **Merge to main** and fly on main: done (see the report's merge commit).
