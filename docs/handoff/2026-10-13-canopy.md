## What changed

- A generated crown-height material supplies local normal relief, irregular
  tone and darker enclosed gaps under high sunlight. It replaces the forest
  floor's albedo/normal detail reads and uses the existing land-cover masks.
  The surface continues past the mesh-tree range and filters into the distance.
- Nearby crowns use a darker palette and smooth normals. High now dissolves
  them over approximately 656–3,281 ft; Medium over 525–2,625 ft. The dissolve
  seed belongs to the site instead of its changing packed-buffer index.
- Trees beyond the vertical visibility range skip both crown and trunk draws.
  Resident placement data remains available for descent; CPU placement is not
  skipped or benchmarked by this experiment.

The first visual pass over-distorted the material into swirls. One correction
batch reduced distortion, closed excessive gaps and added branch/leaf-scale
detail. The final captures above show the corrected material. Terrain height,
collision, coast geometry, simulation and flight HUD are unchanged.

## Reference GPU cost

RX 6700 XT, 2560×1440, High, 100% render scale, noon, clouds off. Exclusive
`ryzen-budget` lock, no overlapping CPU checks or other GPU captures. Windows
counters showed no unrelated active 3D engine immediately before the run;
background video-codec activity remained. Three three-second samples per
state, alternating trees on/off; shader order reversed in the ridge view.
Numbers are median-of-three GPU sample statistics with mesh trees enabled.

| View | Prior p50 | Canopy p50 | Prior p95 | Canopy p95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1,000 ft | 3.492 ms | 3.247 ms | 3.508 ms | 3.266 ms |
| Ridge, 6,000 ft | 3.365 ms | 3.242 ms | 3.646 ms | 4.225 ms |
| 13,000 ft | 3.420 ms | 3.019 ms | 4.393 ms | 3.280 ms |

All sampled p95 values were below the 8.33 ms GPU limit; the highest new-state
p95 was 4.958 ms at the ridge. **Ridge tails worsened and varied:** new p50
samples ranged 3.241–3.446 ms and p95 3.502–4.958 ms. The modest median gain
there is not evidence of a universal performance improvement. At low altitude,
with mesh trees hidden, canopy adds about 0.027 ms; with trees enabled its lower
mesh cost yields a net 0.245 ms median saving. At high altitude it submits zero
trees, compared with 19,165 in the baseline.

The material adds 4 MiB of base texture data, approximately 5.33 MiB with
mipmaps, and a 3,762,533-byte download (about 3.59 MiB). Existing source texture
arrays still load; this prototype does not claim a texture-memory saving.
Raw records and 1440p tree ablations are in
[`2026-10-13-canopy-cost/`](2026-10-13-canopy-cost/).
