## Findings and misses (reported, not tuned)

- **Climb at 25,000 ft reads +55.6%** and is not in the reference. The flown climb had
  cowl flaps open (extra drag); the model has no cowl flaps, so no single `cd0` fits both
  the low and the high altitude points. Fitted for the flown points up to 15,000 ft.
- **The model has one power curve, the normal rating.** Take-off was flown at take-off
  power, so the true take-off gap is not knowable. The +5.3% is against a DERIVED figure.
- **Stalls run 7.6% slow** (the model stalls below the flown, scaled figure); `clMax`
  stays at the shared 1.4. This carries into the ground model: unstick under a firm pull
  lands at 0.931 of 1.1 x the reference stall, below the fighters' 0.95 to 1.15 band.
- **Take-off is long: about 44 s and about 1,100 m from a standstill at test mass**
  (fighters 15 to 25 s). Conformance tests 6 and 8 gave the fighters 40 s and 30 s and
  reported "never left the ground"; they now give the B-17 90 s.
- **`takeoffDistanceM` is ungraded** (its reference is a scaled figure, not a flown roll).
- **The B-17 cannot launch off the Casablanca** (139 m run: leaves the bow on its wheels,
  touches the water). That is D3 (`carrierCapable: false`) behaving as designed; the
  carrier take-off test now covers only carrier-capable aircraft. Nothing else was
  affected (the B-17 was the only `carrierCapable: false` spec). Dev still lists it on a
  carrier start because Dev lists every spec.
- **Power is summed, one engine block, four props.** A failed engine is not modeled.
  Stated in the spec source and on the Library card.
- **Bombs are droppable.** The 8 internal racks carry `an-m65` and the sortie picker offers
  clean and bombs. The bomb falls from inside the hull with no doors, and no rack is
  drawn (D7). Bay doors are a later plan. Real loads went up to 8,000 lb operationally
  (SAC max 12,800 lb); the spec source says so.
- **The process document's D7 differs from Mark's decision.** `docs/aircraft.md` D7 says
  bay loads hang as drawn racks below the aircraft; Mark chose no drawn racks. Reconcile
  when the bay-doors plan is written.
- Eye point is in the canopy only; no cockpit was modeled (D12).
