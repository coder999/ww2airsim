# A3 + A4 handoff: takeoff time and in-flight quality detection (2026-10-09)

Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-a3-a4-launch.md`. Branch `a3a4-launch`, **not merged**: Mark looks first. Unattended run; final-product checkpoint only.

## What changed

**A3: Takeoff time on Form 2 (Sortie Orders).**
- **The field:** a "Takeoff time" field sits under the mission list. It defaults to the scenario's historical hour, read from the same scenario file the briefing loads. The note under it reads "Suggested: 0730, the historical hour. Daylight 0615 to 1745."
- **Daylight clamp:** picks are clamped to daylight, since there is no night lighting. Daylight is computed from the sky's own sun model (`sky/sun.ts` `daylightWindow`): sunrise 0615 and sunset 1745, solar time, at Leyte on the scenario day, rounded inward to the quarter hour.
- **Changing scenario** resets the field to that scenario's hour, as a scenario change already resets the aircraft.
- **The flight:** the picked hour flies, on both a scenario switch and a same-scenario relaunch. DEV `?timeOfDay=` still wins.

**A4: the quality probe.**
- **Where it measures:** the probe now runs in the first seconds of the first sortie, not on the title screen. It skips a warm-up (120 frames or 2 s), then reads up to 240 frames or 5 s, whichever comes first.
- **What it measures:** the median frame interval, not GPU time. **High at 50 fps or better, Medium at 30 fps or better, else Low** (`ocean/tiers.ts` `tierForFrameIntervalsMs`). As before, it is one verdict for ocean, scenery, clouds and effects together, made once, downward only, and never over a player's pick.
- **Recommendation saved:** the verdict is persisted (`ww2airsim.qualityRecommended.v1`), so the "Recommended" stamp survives a reload; until now it was lost. Reset to auto-detect clears it.
- **Quality step in the launch flow:** Form 4 (Ordnance Requisition) gains a **Render quality** row, High / Medium / Low, with "(recommended)" on the verdict. Until the first flight it reads "Not measured yet". A pick there is the same explicit choice as the Settings dialog's.
- **Logging:** the console prints `quality: measured N fps in flight at <tier>; recommends <tier>, in force <tier>`. The GA `quality_tier` event keeps `detected` and `chosen` and gains `fps`.
- **Timestamps:** production no longer tracks GPU timestamps; only the old probe used them there. DEV keeps them for the budget tests.

## What A4 measures on nexus

Radeon 680M, a fresh profile, a Playwright Chromium launched **without** the harness's vsync-off flags, 2560 × 1440:

| Scenario | Probe reads | Verdict | DEV GPU p50 after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free Flight (parked, Tacloban) | 60 fps | High | — |
| Gunnery Range (airborne) | 60 fps | High | 7.1-9.6 ms |
| Combat Air Patrol | 60 fps | High | — |

**The caveat, and it matters.** Headless Chromium does not hold rAF back when the GPU falls behind. At 3840 × 2160 × 1.5 the same flight read **26 ms of GPU per frame** while rAF still ticked every 16.7 ms, so the probe said 61 fps and High. Under the E2E harness's vsync-off flags, rAF runs free and the probe reads 800-1,100 fps.

So on nexus this probe can only ever say High. Headed Chrome does hold rAF back when frames are still pending on the GPU, which is what the design rests on, but **that is not yet measured in Mark's Chrome**. I also tried counting frames the GPU finished (`queue.onSubmittedWorkDone`); it read 454-912 "fps" in the same runs, so it was dropped.

The failure mode is now the opposite of the incident's. A machine that can't hold 50 fps may be judged High where headed Chrome doesn't throttle, where the incident judged Mark's desktop Low. The Form 4 row and Settings are the way out of it.

**To verify:** in Mark's Chrome on the desktop, clear `ww2airsim.quality.v1` and `ww2airsim.qualityRecommended.v1`, fly any sortie, and read the console line. The incident's item 4, a Tier 2 case through `channel: 'chrome'` with no args that expects High, is the permanent form of that check. It needs Ryzen's console session, which tonight's H0 run and the nightly hold, so it isn't built.

## Decisions for Mark (defaults taken)

1. **Latest takeoff is sunset, 1745.** Sim time runs, so a late takeoff flies into dusk with no night lighting. The alternative is ending the window an hour earlier.
2. **The thresholds are 50 and 30 fps.**
3. **The probe still swaps tiers live, once, a few seconds into the first sortie.** The alternative is recommend-only, applied at the next launch.
4. **A Tier 2 Mark's-Chrome assertion** (incident item 4) as the follow-up that validates A4.
