## Provenance — the thing that blocked Task 1

The six WAVs were **generated by Mark with Adobe Firefly** (his answer,
2026-09-18). Recorded in `ASSETS.md` (six rows) and `content/audio/NOTICE.md`.
Adobe grants the generating user commercial use; the notice also records that
the US Copyright Office holds purely AI-generated work uncopyrightable, which
limits what others owe us rather than what we may ship.

`copyContent()` copies `content/` only — so `vite build` shipped **none** of

## The lesson worth carrying

The first Tier 2 run failed two audio cases while the app was working
perfectly. `__ww2` is published early in `boot()` but the keydown listener is
attached hundreds of lines later, so a key sent as soon as `__ww2` exists lands
before anything is listening. Every case now waits for `waitForTerrain` first.
A Tier 2 failure is a claim about the harness as often as about the app —
probe the running page before changing the code.
