## Findings and misses (reported, not tuned)

- **The green cards prove little.** `cd0` 0.020 and `propEfficiency` 0.56 are two unknowns fitted to two
  figures. The stall cards grade the model against a number derived from its own `clMax`, and
  `flap.clIncrement` 0.3565 is derived from that estimate, so it is circular.
- **Stall gap against the source.** The only published stall (120 km/h, 75 mph, no weight or flap state) read
  as a CLEAN stall is 11.7% below the model's 37.2 m/s. The spec reads it as the flaps-down figure because
  it implies CLmax 1.75.
- **Climb conflict.** English Wikipedia says 9.17 m/s, PWE 6.4 m/s (43% lower); neither states altitude
  or weight. The higher figure is graded. Against 6.4 m/s the model would read +43%.
- **Sea-level speed is unchecked.** The model reads 102.6 m/s (230 mph) at sea level; no source exists.
- **Fuel and weight sources conflict.** 4,900 L (JA-WP) used over 3,640 L (PWE, which Dwyer ties to the G6M1);
  9,500 kg "gross" may be a G6M1 figure and is an ESTIMATE for pairing.
- **Engine rating.** `maxPowerW` is the take-off rating from TAIC's Kasei 11 page (summed for two engines); altitude fractions come from its military rating. The spec's `source` string has the derivation.
- **Power is summed, one engine block, two props (`Prop1`, `Prop2`).** A failed engine is not modeled;
  stated in the spec source and on the Library card.
- **Bombs.** No Japanese bomb store exists. Two `an-m65` (907 kg) stand in for the single 800 kg
  bomb (+13% mass), undrawn, in tandem inside the hull, no bay doors. The count is well under the 8-rack cap.
- **Flap type.** JA-WP says slotted flaps; the drawn model has split flaps. Reported, not tuned.
- **Take-off is ungraded** beyond a direction check (`graded.test.ts`: tol 0.1, liftoffMps 45, flapFraction 1); no take-off distance is sourced.
- Eye point is under the glasshouse crown only; no cockpit was modeled (D12).
