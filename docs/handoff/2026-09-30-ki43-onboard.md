## Decisions Mark should look at

- **Gear.** Retractable mains (the model has GearL/GearR rig nodes), fixed tailwheel (no Tailwheel node; ESTIMATE). Geometry is measured
  from the mesh: `heightM` 1.843, `mainX` 0.348, `thirdX` -5.375, `thirdHeightM` 0.595. `travelSeconds` 8 is an ESTIMATE, not the Zero's sourced 10.
  The rig's leg fold angles (-90 and +90 degrees, inboard) are the earlier ESTIMATE and were not re-inspected.
- **Stores.** Two 1,000 lb `an-m65` on racks at z +/-2.4 m, against a historic 2 x 30 to 100 kg (Wikipedia also says 2 x 250 kg). This is
  the least historic number on the airplane; it is labeled Dev stores and disclosed in the spec and on the card. The alternative is guns only.
- **Climb reads +17% to +20%**, the model's known bias (F6F +16.8%, Zero +15.7% to +22.4%). Reported, not tuned.
- **The reference is one intelligence sheet** that other sources contradict (347 mph at 20,000 ft against Wikipedia's 330 mph at 13,000 ft;
  1,105 hp against 1,300 hp). Speeds fit it within 1%, so a green card says the fit works, not that the airplane is right.
- **Flap `clIncrement` 0.389 is circular**: derived from the two ESTIMATED stalls. It is the Zero's number.
- **Stall, roll rate, g limit, control rates, gear and flap travel times, tailwheel steering are ESTIMATES** (Zero and F6F values carried over).
- **The drawn variant is unconfirmed.** The spec uses Ki-43-II figures; the model may be a Ki-43-I.
- **Not modeled:** the differential combat flaps (drawn as an ordinary flap), engine-out, a cockpit model.
- **Not in mission scenarios or AI rosters** (D14); japanese, so the sortie picker offers it in Dev only.
