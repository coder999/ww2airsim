## Take-off roll and lift-off g (measured, not tuned)

- Full throttle from a standstill, flaps up, pull applied from 40 m/s (the conformance harness's control): lifts off at 20.0 s after
  454 m at 45.6 m/s (1.00 x the 1.1 x stall estimate of 45.9 m/s). Conformance passed with the default 40 s window and the default
  liftoff fraction; no widening was needed. The TAIC sheet's 900 ft (274 m) has no stated conditions; the model reads 59% longer
  (435 m to 46 m/s), which the B-29 also does (+29%). Reported, not a fit.
- Lift-off g: the peak load factor from consecutive-tick velocity, 2 s before to 5 s after lift-off, is **1.36 g** (gLimit 4.0), at
  2 s after lift-off. The same probe gives 1.28 g for the B-17 and 1.47 g for the G4M, so it does **not** reproduce the B-17's
  recorded 11.27 g bump; that number came from a different measurement, and I could not tell how. No bump was seen for the Ki-21 in
  this harness; whether the real game shows one was not measured.

## Decisions Mark should look at

- **Gear.** Retractable mains (rig nodes GearL and GearR), fixed tailwheel (ESTIMATE). Mesh-measured `heightM` 2.991, `mainX` 0.547,
  `thirdX` -8.092, `thirdHeightM` 0.942. The tall legs give a 13.3 degree three-point attitude, which no source confirms. The rig
  folds the legs aft; all-aero says forward and it disagrees with historyofwar on whether the wheels were fully enclosed. One
  secondary source each; the rig is left as committed.
- **Stores.** Two 1,000 lb `an-m65` (2,000 lb) on undrawn racks, against a TAIC 1,760 lb normal and 2,200 lb maximum load of 2 to 16
  smaller bombs. Mass matches, count does not. Disclosed on the card and in the spec.
- **`maxTakeoffKg` 10,660** is the TAIC gross, raised from the 10,610 kg secondary maximum because the schema needs `testMassKg` at or under
  it. TAIC prints no maximum.
- **The reference is one intelligence sheet of unknown origin** that other sources contradict (302 mph against 291 mph). cd0 and propulsive
  efficiency are fitted to four figures, so a green card says the fit works, not that the airplane is right.
- **Take-off is not graded**: 435 m against a sourced 274 m with no stated conditions.
- **Power fractions above 15,100 ft (6,000, 9,296 and 11,000 m) are ESTIMATES.**
- **Flap `clIncrement` 0.389 and both stall speeds are ESTIMATES**, circular (the Zero's ratio).
- **The climb check** now follows the reference's own order instead of demanding a fall.
- **Not modeled:** turrets and guns, engine-out, bomb-bay doors, a cockpit model, bomber AI.
- **Not in mission scenarios or AI rosters** (D14); japanese, so the sortie picker offers it in Dev only.
