# F4R: Sherman wear and running tracks

Date: 2026-10-14. Branch `f4r-tracks` (from `f4-night-site-assets`). Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-14-f4r-tracks.md`.
Not merged, nothing deployed. Mark's viewing: final product only (unattended run).

## What Mark asked for, and what was true

1. **Sherman hull detail and wear: done** (`tools/models/blender/m4-sherman.py`).
2. **Tracks of both tanks move like wheels.**
   - **Type 97 Chi-Ha: already did, since V1 (2026-10-09). Nothing about the ingested model changed.** `rigChiHa` spins the hull's 72 wheel shells in a vertex shader and scrolls the separate `Track` mesh's texture by `treadUPerMeter`. The game (`main.ts` `handle.drive(pose.distanceM, ...)` into `rig.drive`) and the Hangar bench (Speed slider) both reach it. This session re-verified it with images (below) and the existing Hangar test, and refactored its wheel shader into a shared helper (`spinWheels`) with identical behavior. Its entry, provenance, credits (Sketchfab `snrnsrk5`, CC-BY-4.0) and input hash are untouched: no derived processing step was needed.
   - **M4 Sherman: new.** Its belt was one static loft inside the joined hull mesh sharing the single skin atlas, so a texture scroll was impossible. It now runs.
   - No shipped scenario drives a tank: Tank Buster's Chi-Has are stationary targets and the Gunnery Range route is a Type 95. So in game today a tank's tracks turn only if a scenario gives it a speed; the render path is wired and tested at the rig level, not exercised by a scenario.

## Sherman running gear

- **Track shoes.** The static belt is replaced by 83 shoes a side (166 in all; plate plus raised grouser, 28 triangles each, 6 in pitch, `LINK_PITCH_M`) in a new kept node `Tracks` (`keep: [{ "node": "Tracks" }]`; one extra draw call). All other node names, pivots, origin and dimensions unchanged. Shoes are identical solids in creation order along the closed path, port side first.
- **Motion.** `rigSherman.drive(distanceM)`: each shoe vertex is blended toward the same vertex of the previous shoe by `fract(distance / pitch)` in the vertex shader (`positionNode = mix(positionLocal, trackPrev, phase)`), so the ground run goes aft as the tank goes forward and one full pitch repeats the picture (wraps forward and backward). The `trackPrev` attribute is derived at load by `trackShoes(geometry)` (welded components in index order; throws if shoes are not identical). Distortion: a shoe shrinks about 4% mid-slide on the sprocket and idler; its normals stay at rest (a `ponytail:` comment in the rig).
- **Wheels.** The hull mesh's round shells (6 road wheels, 3 return rollers, idler, sprocket hub and two tooth rings, per side: 26 shells) spin with the Chi-Ha's shader at distance / radius. Each road wheel and the idler gained five hub bolt heads so the turn is visible (a dual wheel is a body of revolution and shows nothing when it spins). `wheelShells` got an opt-in `exact` mode: every vertex within 10% of the radius (without it, the 0.40 x 0.35 m bogie brackets pass the squareness test and would spin) and fragments standing on a wheel's outboard face (the bolts) join that wheel. The default mode is unchanged: the Chi-Ha still finds 72 shells.
- **Bench.** `VEHICLE_RIGS['m4-sherman']` now offers `parts: ['turrets', 'drive']`, so the Hangar's Speed slider drives it and the game's `vehicle.ts` `drive` reaches it. State read-back: `wheelTravelM`, `wheelShells`, `trackPhase`, `trackShoesPerSide`, `trackPitchM`.

## Sherman wear and detail

Geometry (all small solids share one atlas chart per role; every placement is an ESTIMATE from photographs, labeled in the script): weld beads along the roof-side joint, sponson seam, the cast/weld joint (x = 0.55), the rear corner and the lower-hull plate joints, plus engine deck and rear plate seams; engine deck louvers and access plates; three gasoline cans and a rolled tarpaulin on the engine deck; six spare track blocks and straps on the glacis; a shovel to port and a pick and axe to starboard with clamps; a bent starboard headlamp guard (the one geometric "dent"); on the turret, a loader's hatch ring and a wire basket with a bedroll on the bustle.

Baked into the skin (kit markings, deterministic seed `0x4D34`): mud on the lower hull, sponson edges and running gear; dust on shoes, roof, engine deck and turret top; bare-steel chips along edges and the turret; rust streaks from weld beads; dust-worn rims with dark smudges for dents (texture only: there are no true dents in the mesh); exhaust soot on the rear plate. New marking colors `mudBrown`, `dustTan`, `bareSteel` in `tools/models/skin/colors.ts` (ESTIMATES).

Skin atlas raised 512 to 1024 px (`skin=1024`, entry `textures.maxSize` 1024): at 512 the chips and rust were illegible blobs. Cost: about +75 KB.
