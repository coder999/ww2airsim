# Terrain materials and Tacloban scenery

Continues the local map/compass correction (`29f5319`). North remains -Z;
the terrain and bathymetry binaries and flight model are unchanged.
No remote deployment was made.

## Appearance

- World-locked, mip-filtered procedural sand, grass, soil, jungle canopy and
  exposed rock. Height and slope control transitions; the summit stays
  partly forested. Detail is evaluated per fragment, with existing terrain
  displacement, ocean discard, curvature and aerial perspective preserved.
- Runway mottling and panel seams; draped coral apron and taxiway links.
- Three barrel-roof hangars, a raised control cabin with stairs and aerial,
  three huts, four canvas stores, drums, crates and a windsock. Static
  geometry is merged into eight material batches.
- Nearby trees stream through deterministic 400 m cells, reusing the
  overlapping cells. Two instanced draws, 220 candidates per cell, at most
  26,620 instances each; crowns fade between 1.4 and 1.85 km from the eye.
  Steep slopes, very low shore, runway approaches, apron and river banks
  remain clear. The trees use three low-poly crown lobes each.
- Binahaan and Daguitan centre lines from OpenStreetMap (eight sections,
  49 KB editable JSON). A single shared 4096² single-channel texture is
  generated once, with approximately 8.9 × 6.2 m texels and filtered mipmaps.
  Rivers colour the terrain itself, so they cannot float over LOD changes
  or disagree with the terrain's curvature. This costs about 21 MiB of GPU
  mask storage including mip levels and 16 MiB of retained CPU pixels.

Sources and licenses are recorded in `ASSETS.md` and
`content/scenery/NOTICE.md`. The game displays a linked OSM credit. The
production content directory includes the editable derivative and notice.
`tools/scenery/build.ts` can re-extract the cached public search responses;
it makes no network calls. The runtime uses only bundled data.

## Scope limits

This is a first surface-detail pass, not completion of the entities roadmap.
Buildings and trees have no collision, damage or gameplay behavior. The
base arrangement is period-inspired, not an archival reconstruction. Material
and tree placement are procedural, not ESA WorldCover classifications.
The coast still has the coarse L4 DEM's shape; texture detail cannot restore
missing shoreline geometry. Rivers have illustrative constant widths (38/46 m),
use modern mapped courses, and follow the DEM surface without carving channels
or changing ground/water contact. Only the two named rivers are included;
upstream data has gaps. River water has static colour detail, not flowing waves.

## Verification

- TypeScript, ESLint, dependency-cruiser and production build pass.
- Full unit suite: 911 passed, 11 failed, 6 skipped. The same 11 failures
  were recorded before scenery edits: nine Windows architecture harness
  process/symlink cases, one exact floating-point golden comparison, one
  CRLF-sensitive header checksum. No tolerances or goldens changed.
- All 40 focused terrain/runway/geography/scenery checks pass. New cases
  exercise building clearance on real terrain, deterministic vegetation,
  clearing stale instances over open ocean, and the actual river mask's
  coordinate orientation and bank exclusion.
- Actual WebGPU views inspected in the in-app browser on AMD RDNA-2:
  parked runway/base, airborne base, river/forest and upland terrain. Browser
  error logs were empty. Displayed frame rates were about 99–101 fps at
  1280×720. These are observed frame rates, not a formal GPU benchmark.
- The full Playwright Tier 2 suite was not run.

## Repeatable local views

Run `npm run dev -- --port 5175` and open `http://127.0.0.1:5175/`.
The bare URL is the normal parked flight. Development-only `sceneryView=1`
starts paused and points the chase eye 36 degrees down. It combines with the
existing spawn overrides; none of these inspection controls ship in production.

- Base: `?spawnX=-30250&spawnY=260&spawnZ=-47605&sceneryView=1`
- Binahaan: `?spawnX=-40900&spawnY=700&spawnZ=-30666&sceneryView=1`
- Upland forest: `?spawnX=-65000&spawnY=1900&spawnZ=-32000&sceneryView=1`

Reload the bare URL to resume ordinary camera behavior.
