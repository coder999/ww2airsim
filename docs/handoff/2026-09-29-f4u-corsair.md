## What was accepted, and gaps against the detail spec

- **Stall runs about 5% fast.** `clMax` stays at the shared 1.4. The DS quotes 89.0 mph
  "without power" with no flap condition named, so the stall targets come from the flown
  FG-1A (TED BIS 2122) scaled by the square root of the weight ratio, labeled DERIVED.
  Fitting `clMax` to close 5% would be tuning to the card.
- **Climb is 3% low.** Within tolerance. Not chased.
- **Flap and lift-off data are missing from the DS**, so they come from the FG-1A trial,
  cited as a supplement. The flap increment is the shared formula
  (`clIncrement = 1.400013 x ((Vclean/Vflap)^2 - 1)`).
- **Ground angle.** The drawn model rests at 10.27 degrees; the DS static angle is
  10 deg 59 min. The gearContact test holds the derived rest pitch to the drawn stance
  within 0.25 degrees, and it does.
- **Model is an F4U-1A airframe** (raised canopy, matches the -1D's). The
  `eyePoints` test passes with the glazing node `kabina-FACES_1` (x -2.478 to -1.169 m,
  top 1.075 m): the canopy finding is that the eye point sits inside the glass.
  No load-time modification was made. `ASSETS.md` line 23 credits the model.
- **Engine torque yaw is 0** (Mark's ruling, 2026-09-28); prop wash is 12 m/s.
- **Carrier.** `carrierTakeoff.test.ts` passes for the Corsair on both decks
  (casablanca-cve, essex-cv) at flaps 0 and 1 with no exception-list entry and no
  threshold change.
- Stores: 2 racks (an-m65) and 8 HVAR rails, offsets measured, not estimated.

## Process lessons

- Stall targets are the hard part with a guarantee: find a flown trial in the same
  family and derive, do not fit `clMax`.
- `wildcatMounts.test.ts` covers every stores-carrying spec: add the new id to its list.
- `centerPoint.test.ts` needs a per-model STATION when gear legs sit at the 30% station.
- Adding a flyable changes the picker order pins in `sortieFlow.test.ts` and
  `sortie/flyable.test.ts`, and the Hangar catalog tests that used it as the
  "not in the game" fixture (now the P-38).
- Every flyable needs `spec` on its Library card, or `aircraftSide.test.ts` fails.
- `remote-run npm run verify` stops at the first failing stage; run the later stages
  separately to see everything.
- Tier 2 on nexus needs `sg render -c` or it falls to SwiftShader and the guard page
  appears. A `timeout` shorter than the suite kills it without saying so.
