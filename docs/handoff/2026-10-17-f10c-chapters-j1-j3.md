## Findings (measured 2026-10-17, ryzen, headless; ruling needed where marked)

1. **J1's raid cannot threaten three groups, so `strike` cannot fail by AI action alone.** Eight variants (altitude 1,800 and 3,000 m; approach from the east and 60 degrees either side; both waves at once; with and without the wingmen and Ki-43s; the player passive and 60 km away): the six attackers destroyed 0 to 2 of the five groups, and the fuel farm was the usual loss. Each attacker dropped 0 or 1 bomb: Buri's three AAA emplacements and the ground kill most before they drop. A passive player who takes off and lands earns the badge. Pinned as a tripwire (`MEASURED GAP`), not fixed: geometry and counts are all F3 and Decision 8 leave to tune, and the counts are locked. **Ruling for Mark:** accept, or add a player-credit objective (a `destroy` count of raiders), or retune the AI's bombing.
2. **No AI aircraft can attack a vehicle** (`src/sim/ai` has no vehicle target; `pilot.target` takes aircraft ids; ingress `attack` takes an airfield or a ship). In J3 the P-38s dive-bomb Buri's dispersal and fight the Ki-43s; with a passive player 0 of 13 vehicles were lost at 700 s and the column drove into the zone at 443 to 480 s with no player action (`delivered` completes). Only the Bayug landing is the player's own. Tripwire test, so the day the AI learns to attack vehicles it goes red and J3 is re-measured. This is also A3's problem: the same gap applies to the P-38 side. **Ruling for Mark / a later engine task.**
3. **J2's weapon margin.** The Kate carries one Type 91 (damage 102, plus a flood of 0.6 x that over 60 s). One hit takes a Fletcher (160) to 58, below half at once; a Maru (240) ends at 77 and a Cleveland (360) at 197 (0.55, above half). F3's "destroy one Maru or take a Fletcher or Cleveland below half" is therefore reachable only through a Fletcher with the weapon the player has (a second hit would do the others). Pinned by arithmetic from the content. **Ruling for Mark:** accept (the Fletcher is the intended target), raise the threshold, or give the Kate a second weapon.
4. **J2's CAP timing.** A straight 75 m/s run from the start: the first F6F inside 6 km at 57 s, merging near 100 s, the penetration station complete at 160 s. F3 says CAP 2 to 3 minutes; met at the penetration. An attempt to push the CAP out made it never detect the player (they fly off), so it stays. Ships at 24 km, 5 to 6 minutes.
5. **J2's gate.** 20 km west of the shipping (world -46500, -41000, radius 3,000 m), 269 s from the first action at 75 m/s against a 300 s budget; the fit row now carries it (`landingS: 0`, since nothing lands).
6. **J1's runway rule.** Nothing models a runway crater, so Buri's serviceability is a `fieldRules` rule: unserviceable once two of the tower, ammunition bunker and barracks are destroyed (0.5 of three). Invented; chosen so the three are NOT among the five tracked groups.
7. **J3's AAA count.** F3 says two destination AAA; Buri's window has three. Not changed (the site is F8B's).
8. **The takeoff run.** The Zero lifts off from the engine-running start (runway-local -8, -330, 430 m of strip ahead) in the real `deckRun`; the chapter's fit link (58 m from the fit row's parked spot) holds.
