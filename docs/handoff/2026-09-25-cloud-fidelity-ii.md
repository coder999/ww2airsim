# Cloud Fidelity II handoff

2026-09-25. The design at
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-cloud-fidelity-ii-design.md` is implemented
on the isolated `worktree-cloud-fidelity-ii` branch. It is intentionally not
merged, pushed, or deployed; choosing whether this visual direction lands on
`main` remains Mark's call.

## What changed

- `5434130` removes the scene-wide motion MRT. Cloud reprojection reconstructs
  camera motion from depth at quarter linear resolution; full-resolution TRAA
  keeps depth/disocclusion rejection.
- `8318d0b` updates one of sixteen High-tier cloud pixels per frame and carries
  history through the other fifteen, neighborhood-clamped to the current 3x3
  fresh samples. That budget funds 128 view steps and six near-light samples,
  two of them using detailed density.
- `874d7b7` replaces the old coverage modulation with a deterministic 512²
  RGBA weather map: one Worley cell per cloud, carrying coverage potential,
  type, top height, and cell peak. Cumulus now has separate footprints, a
  common flat base, and varied tops.
- The final §3.4 commit packs a 128³ Perlin-Worley + three-octave RGBA shape
  volume, a 64³ three-octave RGBA detail volume, and a 128² RG curl field.
  Shape texels span 20 m; detail repeats over 40 m; curl displaces only detail
  coordinates. All generated bytes are committed and SHA-256 pinned.

The final renderer allocates about 10 MiB raw for these cloud assets: 8 MiB
shape, 1 MiB detail, 1 MiB weather, and 32 KiB curl. Detail uses RGBA rather
than RGB because WebGPU/Three's portable 3D texture path supports RGBA8; only
RGB carry data and A is fixed padding.

## Visual and coverage result

The fixed runway, photo-reference, under-deck, in-deck, and above-deck captures
in `.superpowers/sdd/cloud-fidelity-ii/shots/3.4-final/` were read directly and
compared with §3.3. The smooth §3.3 blocks gained smaller billows, broken
silhouettes, and darker internal folds; flat bases and per-cell top variation
remain visible, with no sparkle, black frames, validation errors, or console
errors.

Cloud-pixel fraction in the fixed coverage regions is 0.701 above the deck,
0.668 below it, 0.534 in the photo view, and 0.521 from the runway. The
above-deck result is 10% below the 0.775 pre-weather baseline, inside the
design's ±20% acceptance band.

## Reference-GPU evidence

Windows RX 6700 XT through `PW_REMOTE=ws://localhost:39001/`, with nexus Vite
served through the existing localhost reverse tunnel:

- Fixed capture set: 5/5, inspected; zero validation/console errors.
- 4K budget: 18/18. High p95 8.604-9.716 ms against 16.67 ms; Medium p95
  5.692-6.917 ms against 8.33 ms.
- Clouds, shadows, temporal accumulation, and motion: 24 functional cases
  passed. Cloud roll, TRAA roll, teleport, restart, look snap, and camera-cut
  captures are clean; reprojection residuals retain a 3.4-5.9x advantage over
  reversed motion during the pull-up.
- Camera-motion reconstruction remains 3.639 ms p50 in the 4K clouds-off
  in-deck probe.

The legacy 1440p differential micro-budgets were written before Mark moved High
to 60 Hz. The added packed fetch/curl work measured 6.07-6.20 ms total, while
independent-load deltas varied too much to remain useful upper bounds. Their
total-frame tripwire is now 8.33 ms; `budget4k.spec.ts` remains the explicit,
authoritative High/Medium gate.
