### What each one has now

- **CCKW:** closed cab with split windshield, door glass and handles, mirrors, running boards; hood with hinge strip and
  a white star; radiator shell with grille slats; fenders with real wheel arches; rear wheel tunnel over the tandem; steel
  bumper with winch roller, tow hooks and headlamps; bed with timber floor, ribbed sides, rub rails, tailgate, headboard;
  canvas cover over bows; frame rails, cross members, three axles with differentials, springs, driveline, tank, muffler;
  six treaded wheels with dished hubs. Doors carry stars.
- **D7:** two tracks of about 45 individually cleated shoes each; five bottom rollers and two carriers a side; flanged idler
  with yoke and recoil spring; toothed sprockets; hood with louvers, radiator, grille, lamps, exhaust stack (the 1.90 m
  height) and air cleaner; seat, levers and pedals; fuel tank; drawbar; push arms, cross shaft, trunnions, hydraulic lift
  rams with hoses, tilt braces; moldboard blade with cutting edge, ribs, beams and end plates.
- **Kurogane:** arched fenders, louvered hood, cowl and instrument panel, tub with seats front and rear, folded canvas top
  and bows, spare wheel on the rear panel, grille and bumper, lamps, folded-forward windscreen frame (the 1.68 m height),
  a segmented steering wheel on the existing pivot, chassis with axles, springs, exhaust and skid plate, treaded wheels.

## Things that changed beyond the meshes (read these)

1. **Wheel and blade pivots.** The previous CCKW and D7 entries had no `normalize`, so their nodes had no pivots and
   `rigSimpleWheeled` / `rigDozer` turned every wheel and track about the world origin. Both entries now `normalize`
   (identity transform at the cited length) and give each wheel, sprocket and blade node a `pivot`. `tests/render/vehicleRig.test.ts`
   has new cases proving each wheel and sprocket keeps its center under `drive` and the blade rises with its arms.
2. **One line in `src/render/scene/vehicleRig.ts`:** `rigDozer` now does `raise.attach(tilt)`. The two blade nodes were
   siblings, so raising the arms left the blade behind. Node names, axes, limits and `state()` keys are unchanged.
   Revert that line if you want the rig byte-for-byte as it was; the new D7 test then fails on "the blade rose".
3. **Track motion.** `TrackL` and `TrackR` are the sprockets (the toothed wheel the rig already turns at 0.36 m radius),
   not the whole belt. A rigid belt cannot turn about a point, and the old rig turned a slab. The shoes are static. A
   scrolling shoe animation would be a new rig feature.
4. **Draw calls.** The CCKW is 9, not 8: its eight kept nodes each draw, and its steel, glass, wood, canvas and chassis
   parts join into one more. `tests/tools/models/vehicleModels.test.ts` has a per-vehicle `DRAW_CALLS` ceiling (CCKW 9;
   everything else stays 8) and the entry budget says 9. Triangle and byte budgets are unchanged (20,000 / 1 MB).
5. **Wheel hubs are painted, not separate nodes.** A second role inside a kept node costs a draw (measured 7 -> 8 on a
   trial build), so the hub is a dished dome in the wheel's own material, colored by a `hub` disc marking.
6. **New capture tool** `tests/e2e/f4rGroundCaptures.spec.ts` (listed in `captureTools.ts`).

## Known gaps

- The stars and hub paint are soft at the 512 px skin (the CCKW's atlas shares texels among about 150 parts).
- The hydraulic ram bases stay on the hood while the rams ride the push arms, so at full raise (30 degrees) a ram base
  separates by roughly 0.2 m.
- The CCKW wheels are single, not dual; dual rear tires are not in the node contract.
- Nothing here changes gameplay placement of these vehicles.
