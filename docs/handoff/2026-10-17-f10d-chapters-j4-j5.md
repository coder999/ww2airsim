## Plan corrections

- The plan said a recorded drop needs a "format version bump". It is additive instead (as F5C's ground stop and F6A's lights): version 1 stays; reasoning in `docs/flight-recorder.md`.
- "`ingress.attack: 'airdrop'`" does not reuse E2's attack machinery: the schema maps it to `IngressOrders.airdrop`; `attack.ts` never sees it.
- `drop {targets: player and one AI, count: 2}` is two objectives (`drop-player`, then `drop-ai`) because a count cannot name "the player".
- The AI Ki-57s cannot be slot wingmen (`leader` excludes `ingress`); they are route-flown and drop on their own order.
- Scheduled lights ran on the WORLD tick; an opening scene shifts the mission clock, so J4's flare and its window would have been 15 s apart. Fixed engine-side (`NightFacts.clockOriginTick`).

AI drop altitude is about 400 m above the ground because the AI safety floor never lets an AI lower (measured: 150 m start climbs to 501 m on 100 m ground). Real drops were lower; left as is.
