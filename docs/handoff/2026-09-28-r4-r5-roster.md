# R4 + R5 handoff: buildings and vehicles (2026-09-28)

R4 and R5 finish the model roster: every Library building and vehicle has its
own model in the Hangar, and the not-yet-drawn allowlist is gone. This work
changes no scenario and does not replace the procedural airfield buildings in
the game. The design is
[`2026-09-26-model-roster-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-model-roster-design.md),
and the combined plan is
[`2026-09-27-r4-r5-buildings-vehicles.md`](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-27-r4-r5-buildings-vehicles.md).

The work is complete on branch `worktree-r4-r5-roster`. Mark asked for that
branch to be merged into `main` on 2026-09-28. The branch's final R4/R5 commit
range before this handoff is `0c19b79`..`9450b93`; earlier R4 building-kit and
building commits had already reached `main` while the combined work was in
progress.

## What shipped: R4 buildings

The Blender kit now has outward-wound `frustum`, `gable_roof`, `tank`,
`sandbag_ring`, `strut`, `gun_barrel`, and `lattice_mast` parts, plus the
`earth` palette role. R2's `cylinder`, `tapered_box`, and `turret` remain
unused here because their faces wind inward; correcting them would change
R2's committed ship GLBs. Per-island signed-volume tests and argument probes
cover every new part.

The Willys download contains two complete jeeps. Mark chose the open jeep with
its top down and windshield folded; the canvas-top jeep was removed. A -5.7°
yaw squares its axles. The retained model's source wheelbase is
`W = 1.773005`, its source length is `L = 2.947000`, and the cited 80 in
(2.032 m) wheelbase gives `L × 2.032 / W = 3.377489 m`, stored as 3.377 m.

`vehicleModels.test.ts` is the vehicle Tier 1 harness. It measures every
committed vehicle against its dated citation; checks ground contact, footprint
centering, +x-forward geometry, the 1 MB / 20,000-triangle / 8-draw budget,
and exact turret names. Turning either manifest 180° was proven to fail its
own geometry-based forward check.

The final roster assertions are direct: every Library entry resolves through
the display loader, and every building and vehicle names a model. There is no
allowlist or ceiling left.

### Vehicle rulings

- **V1:** the vehicle draw-call budget is 8; §4.4 already supplies 1 MB and
  20,000 triangles.
- **V2:** vehicle width tolerance is 6%; length and height are per row. The
  length-fitted Chi-Ha uses an 8% height row because the source is +7.5% tall,
  all at the turret top.
- **V3:** a vehicle stands on y=0, centered on its footprint; a separable
  turret is `Turret1`, with no pivot yet.
- **V4:** the jeep is fitted by its cited 2.032 m wheelbase and its overall
  length is checked to 4%.
- **V5:** connected components on the +x side of the squared source become
  `CanvasJeep` and are removed.
- **V6:** the wheelbase-exact jeep is 1.670 m wide, +6.4% against 1.57 m, so
  its measured row uses 7% rather than disturbing the authoritative fit.

## Departures and findings

- The existing work was found on legacy branch `worktree-r4-radio-radar`;
  after removing a verified-empty checkout, it was renamed to the planned
  `worktree-r4-r5-roster` branch rather than replaying completed commits.
- The plan's radio/radar Blender name filter matched no current test name;
  the current `radio-radar-station$` filter ran the intended byte-identical
  rebuild.
- `content/vehicles/` did not yet exist. The Chi-Ha's first build failed
  `ENOENT`; creating the planned output directory fixed it.
- The plan's centroid-distance Chi-Ha muzzle proof selected the offset
  turret's rear corner by 0.037 m. The shipped proof compares the complete
  `Turret1` forward and aft extensions and fails a 180° mutation.
- Task 13's broader transitional run retained one obsolete not-drawn fixture
  after the allowlist emptied; Task 14 immediately replaced it with a
  synthetic no-model entry and restored the full set to green.
- The jeep's measured width required V6, recorded above. No geometry or
  citation was hidden by widening the shared tolerance.
