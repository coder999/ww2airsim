## Findings and misses (reported, not tuned)

- **ESTIMATE, no source:** lift-off speed (the manual says 115 to 130 mph; 54.8 m/s is the midpoint), roll rate, `gLimit`,
  gear and flap travel times, `cd0`, `propEfficiency`, pitch and yaw rates.
- **Take-off reads 29% long** (1,073 m against 830.6 m). At a 115 mph lift-off it reads 911 m (+9.6%), so the lift-off speed sets
  the miss, not the engine (a 2,200 hp sea-level run is only 4% shorter). In the running game the airplane lifted off at
  128 mph, inside the manual's range.
- **Ground conformance** needs a 110 s window: 90 s never lifted off and 100 s did, at 1.1 x the model's own stall.
- **cd0, propEfficiency and the 7,620 m power fraction are fitted:** ten figures, three unknowns. The manual's speed and climb charts
  disagree at 25,000 ft (0.85 fits the speed, 0.88 the climb; 0.87 is the compromise).
- **Stalls read 3% fast** because the shared `clMax` 1.4 is not this wing's (the manual implies about 1.49).
- **Bomb load: 8 x 1,000 lb = 8,000 lb modeled against a 20,000 lb historic maximum** (Wikipedia). The schema caps racks at 8 and D8
  allows no new store. A game limit, not a claim about the airplane.
- **The drawn gear stands about 6 feet (1.8 m) too tall** (10.3 m drawn against 8.46 m real). The drawn main wheels also sit at the swept
  wing's root quarter-chord, so `centerPoint.test.ts` holds this model to 0.20 m, not 0.15 m (the drawing is not edited, D4).
- **Engine-out is not modeled:** one engine block, four propellers turning.
- **Sources:** the PFOI has no stable URL recorded (found through a web search of a scan). The Library card lists it as a title, with the
  Wikipedia URL as the link, and says so.
- Eye point is under the glazed crown only; no cockpit was modeled (D12). The five turrets are drawn, do not move, and do not fire.
