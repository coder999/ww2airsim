## Findings that changed the plan (the plan's numbers were claims; these are measured)

1. **The Betty carries one torpedo, no bomb** (`g4m-betty.json`). The plan's level-bombing Bettys never attacked (`attackFlight` returns null for a kind the rack cannot carry). They are `torpedo` raiders; Vals dive-bomb. Ordnance: one type91 does 102 (163 with its flood, 60 percent), one Val bomb 48. **Four Bettys and two Vals sink at most two 240-HP Marus**, and only with two torpedoes on each; measured unopposed (player 100 km away, 780 s): one Maru sunk, one at 77 of 240, two untouched.
2. **A ship screen beside the transports (Fletcher, Cleveland) killed the whole raid with nobody in the air**: the player would have been handed the objective. The screen stands 13.5 to 15.5 km seaward.
3. **A2: the plan's geometry (Japanese 8 km from the screen) killed both Fletchers by 120 s and Casablanca by 300 s unaided, and the friendly Fletchers' fire alone took Mogami from 420 to 183** (it would have completed the damage objective for the player). Japanese ships now stand 17 to 24 km off, the screen is ordered onto Yamato; passive: no Japanese hull is hurt, Casablanca is untouched until about 600 s and sinks about 1,000 s.
4. **A3's mission-fit first-action point (-65478, -28976) is a 644 m ridge**; no column drives there. The column drives the low plain north of Ormoc (heights 15 to 110 m). The start, contact zone and fit row moved with it.
5. **A3 cannot load in the loader's default windows**: Tacloban is Japanese and unserviceable in `pre-invasion` and `assault`, and a landing objective there is refused at load. It loads only after A2's transition (proved in `a3-the-long-column.test.ts` from the real reducers). Fix for every loader: `loadScenarioBundle(id)` with no windows now builds a **chapter** scenario in its chapter's play windows (`tools/content/load.ts`). A5 (batch B) needs the same and gets it.
6. **The F10-0 contract (`friendly.test.ts`) wants a suggested recovery on every chapter**, so A3's `land` is `suggestedRecovery` at Tacloban: **any friendly base counts, a serviceable Dulag also earns the badge** (F3 names Tacloban only; ruling below).
7. Ships carry flooding: a torpedo hit does its damage plus 0.6 of it over 60 s (`flooding.ts`). One Mk 13 (136) is 217.6: Mogami to 202 of 420, Yamato to 982 of 1,200.

## Rulings for Mark (defaults taken)

1. **A1 `protect` is `maxLost: 1` (F3: three of four survive).** The raid cannot sink more than two Marus and unopposed sank one, so the transports objective is hard to fail from the air. `maxLost: 0` would make the defense bite. Default: F3.
2. **A1 shows a helper objective, "Bombers reach release range" (secondary), because a `until` window cannot name an AI arrival.** It completes when a bomber is within 3 km of the anchorage; a player sees a secondary complete that is bad news.
3. **A1's Cleveland is present and silent.** Main batteries fire only at ships and structures, never at inland points. A shore bombardment needs a primitive that does not exist.
4. **A2's `land` is `after: hit`, not after the window**, so the player may trap as soon as the hit is made; the shield is judged at the trap (a standing `protect` that has not failed reads complete). The window ends at 540 s.
5. **A3 accepts any friendly base** (finding 6). F3's "stop/destroy" stays destroyed-only (the plan's ruling; no immobilize state exists).
6. **A3's AAA is a noStrip base** (`a3-road-aaa`), the only way a scenario can place a firing emplacement off an airfield.
7. **F3's "no transition on A2" is amended** (spec section 4, A2, and `worldStates.ts`).
8. The demo scenes `demo-opening` and `demo-victory` are **left in place**: five batches would race to delete the same two files and their test references. Delete them in one commit when the first chapter merges (Decision 3).
