## Decisions and deviations (Mark may overrule)

1. **A site without a strip is an `Airfield` with `noStrip: true`** rather than a schema change to make `runway` optional (27 consumers). Nothing about the three flying-field kinds changed; absent in every shipped file.
2. **Anchorage recovery stays per scenario.** F5C's comment said F8 might replace `Scenario.anchorages` with base data; that needs the player's side at expansion time, so Jinamoc only supplies coordinates (the A4 row checks the site is allied, serviceable and lit). Follow-up for F10.
3. **San Pedro Bay is `jinamoc`** (world key and airfield id); `built: false` in `pre-invasion` (F3: Japanese waters, nothing to place). The shipped terrain has one islet in San Pedro Bay (about 280 x 300 m) and the site is on it, 2.5 km from Tacloban, not the Navy's 5 mi.
4. **Ormoc and the beaches follow the terrain's coast, not the real one** (Ormoc about 0.8 km east of the real city; one representative beachhead, the X Corps sector between Palo and Tacloban, labeled inferred; the Dulag beaches are not drawn).
5. **A1 plays the beaches in `assault` and its success writes `buildup`** (F3 section 4's literal "advances from assault to established beachhead"), while the fields stay `pre-invasion` at 0800 as F8A ruled.
6. **`serviceable` for a site without a strip means usable for its job**: Ormoc always true; beaches false (pre-invasion, alternate), true otherwise; Jinamoc false (assault, alternate), true in the build-up and endgame; the alternate reads "abandoned" as allied-and-unusable.
7. **`credit()` change.** One added field in the object `credit()` returned, in the branch that already appends the chapter. No scoring, ledger, attempt, roster or call-order change; no `schemaVersion` bump (`world` was already in schema 2); no recorder change. A replayed or out-of-order chapter never writes the world.
8. The plan said "reuse Tasks 1-9 unchanged": I extended `expandScenario`'s output only through the new flag, `airfield.ts` (slot height, huts, clearing), `structures.ts` (seat) and `vehicles.ts` (`floats`). Tacloban's F8A pier has no `seatM` (the seat requirement is tested for the F8C sites only); it floats a little and could take `seatM: 0`.
9. `tools/sites/localMap.ts` is new: ground and layout in a site's own frame (used to fit all three frames).
