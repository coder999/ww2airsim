## Why

AI-vs-AI fights don't end in kills. That blocks Scramble, Escort, M2's AA fire and E3's gunners. The causes are on record (7c spec, "Added to the gunnery-honesty follow-on"; 7e handoff R-F1):

- The AI leads by the **target's** velocity. Rounds inherit the shooter's velocity, so the lead must be on the **relative** velocity.
- It ignores **drop**, so a perfect aim streams about 7 ft under a straight-flying target. That is why the veteran's control noise was turned down to keep it harmless (Mark, 2026-09-25).
- There is **no explicit per-skill aim error**. Lethality came from accidents of noise.

## Scope (from MASTER_PLAN)

1. Lead on relative velocity, with the round's drag on its inherited part, and with drop compensation.
2. A per-skill aim error: higher is worse, the `controlNoise` convention. It is drawn at every rescore and held until the next one.
3. Re-run 7c §3.1's lethality instruments. Re-express the point-blank break-off with the pursuer's guns made safe, so it tests the break-off rather than lethality (7c spec Decisions item 2).

## Instrument: measured, not guessed

`tools/ai/duel.ts`: AI-vs-AI 1v1 duels of two F6Fs through production `advance`. There are three geometries (head-on at 1.9 mi, tail at 650 yd, crossing), three skill pairings and the four spec §3.1 noise cursors, each run for 180 s.

**Baseline (main, 2026-10-09): 1 kill in 36 duels, and 1 hit per 150 rounds.**

## Tasks

1. The duel harness. Measure the baseline.
2. Fix the solution (`pursuit.ts` `muzzleLeadDirection`): relative velocity, drag and drop. Add a test that one round fired along the solution meets a straight-flying target in five geometries.
3. Perception: the pilot dead-reckons its last observation along the observed velocity until the next rescore. This was found while probing: a veteran's 0.3 s stale snapshot put its aim about 120 ft behind a target flying 270 mph.
4. Fine aim. Within 12° of the solution, a tracking law commands body rates: the solution's own rotation rate fed forward, plus a correction. This was also found while probing: the velocity controller's proportional pitch left a standing 3.4-5° error in a 2 g turn, outside the veteran's 1.8° cone.
5. Per-skill aim error and tracking strength. Tune them so the veteran is deadlier than green, and so the 7d bar still holds (a scripted evasion gets behind green).
6. Re-express the lethality tests (`aiLethality.test.ts` items 1 and 2), and enroll the duel test (`tests/sim/ai/gunnery.test.ts`).
7. Run the full suite on ryzen and the combat E2E locally. Write the handoff.

## Acceptance

- One round fired along the solution passes within 1 ft of a straight-flying target in five geometries: tail, abeam, 30° deflection, head-on and climbing.
- The veteran-involved duel rows end in kills in at least 3 of 4 runs per row, and a veteran on a green's tail kills it inside 30 s every time.
- Veteran deadlier than green: on a passive player, the veteran's kill tick is earlier than green's at every cursor and loadout.
- The 7d bar holds: the scripted evasion gets behind a green pursuer within 40 s at all 16 runs, with the player alive.
- Everything else stays green.

## Decisions for Mark (defaulted; not blocking)

1. **Lethality against a passive player.** With honest gunnery, a veteran on a passive player's tail at 550 yd kills in 2.6-3.4 s; green kills in 9 of 16 runs, in 7-19 s. That reverses the 2026-09-25 "tone the veteran down" result, which was really a symptom of the broken aim. Default: keep it honest, and leave overall difficulty to M5 (Settings).
2. **Green does not fine-track** (`gunTracking 0`). With half-strength tracking (0.3 or 0.5), the 7d bar fails in 2 of 16 runs, against your ruling that "green pilots should be beaten easily". Cost: green rarely kills a maneuvering opponent (5 of 24 green-v-green duels).
3. **Dead-reckoned perception** changes 7d's "aims where the target used to be" to "misses only the target's maneuvers since the last look".
