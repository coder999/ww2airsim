## What was asked (Mark, 2026-10-10, and MASTER_PLAN Track M item 2)

- Ships and ground `aaa` structures share one AA system.
- Heavy guns throw **timed flak bursts at altitude** (a burst detonates near the target and damages in a radius with falloff). Light guns **fire tracer rounds through the existing ballistics at close range**.
- Builds on E1's lead and aim error, with per-mount skill instead of perfect aim.
- **Both sides fire, every gun**, at any airplane of the opposing side in range, under the friendly-fire rules. Never their own side.
- **Default lethality: MODERATE**, as a measured, tested calibration: lingering low (about 300 ft) over a destroyer costs the player a Hellcat in roughly 20 s; a straight fast pass at that height usually survives. Every tuning number lives in one named config object (M5 will scale it).
- Out of scope: M3 (gun-laying AI) and M4 (main batteries). A simple director behind a seam. Ship main guns never fire at ships. Flooding and torpedoes untouched.
- Determinism: no AA mounts present means bit-identical results.

## Design

**New file `src/sim/weapons/aaFire.ts`** (pure, no PRNG state):
- `AA_TUNING`: the one named config (ranges, rates, errors, damage, blast radius and peak, caps). Comments say what each moves; M5 scales it.
- `mountsOf(spec)`: the armament flattened to mounts (tier, caliber, ship-frame position, barrels), cached per spec; a structure of kind `aaa` is one heavy and one light mount.
- `stepAa(state, view)`: for each armed, live ship or structure with a hostile airborne airplane in range, each ready mount picks the nearest hostile in its envelope and fires. Randomness is a hash of (seed, mount, time window), so the same world replays the same and a world with no AA never draws.
- Light: one tracer round per salvo through the existing ballistics, aimed with the lead solve plus a per-mount aim error (redrawn each second) and per-round dispersion. A round stands for the mount's salvo (hit scale times barrels, capped).
- Heavy: a shell is not flown. The director solves a constant-speed intercept, adds angular and fuse-time error, and schedules a burst at the aimed point for the time of flight. At the due tick it damages every hostile airplane within the radius by `peak * (1 - d / radius)`, structure only, credited to the owner.
- State (`CombatState.aa`): pending bursts, per-mount next-fire ticks, the light-gun-firing list the audio reads, and the seed.

**`stepCombat`** gains two small call sites: AA fire before the projectile loop, and a branch for AA rounds inside it. Both do nothing when no mount has a target.

**Cost bounds:** a ship-level range early-out before any mount is touched; only airborne hostile airplanes (speed over 20 m/s, not burning or down) are candidates; a cap on live AA rounds and on pending bursts; AA rounds test only hostile airplanes (a sphere broad phase first), the sea and terrain.

**Existing missions:** every scenario with an armed ship or `aaa` structure changes when an enemy airplane comes into range (Combat Air Patrol, Convoy Strike's Kagero escort, Airfield Strike's Dulag battery, the Range Test). That is the intended effect; each affected test is re-run and re-pinned only with a stated reason (handoff).

- AA fire at parked airplanes, or from a sinking ship.

## Rulings (defaults taken unattended; recorded in the handoff)

- Light mounts fire one aggregate round per salvo, weighted by barrels (capped), instead of one round per barrel.
- Flak shells are not flown; the burst point is fixed at fire time (a maneuvering target can fly out of it).
- Airborne means over 20 m/s.
- A `aaa` structure is one heavy and one light mount.
- The Range Test's ships shoot the player; God mode makes it harmless.
