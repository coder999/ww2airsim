- **D, cards.** A `CARDS` entry with tolerances that state the misses; the card's climb-direction check now follows a sourced rising table.
- **E, model and register.** Committed glb and rig unchanged (the rig has no gear nodes, P15); stance, center point, eye point (`Object_20`).
- **F, fit the drawing.** Gear and belly rack measured from the model; guns and zones checked by `combatFit.test.ts`.
- **G, Tier 2.** `flyableAll.spec.ts -g d3a`, `hangar.spec.ts`, `sortie.spec.ts`.
- **H, flyable in the game.** Selectable in the sortie UI in Dev, parks, takes off, drops a bomb.
