## Chapter notes

**J1 Defend the Fields** (A6M2, 0830 partly cloudy). The player starts engine-running at Buri with one A6M2; two Ki-43 arrive after takeoff (a held group released on `completed: takeoff`). Five tracked groups: fuel farm, radio station, north hangar, south hangar, parked-aircraft dispersal (tag sets on the Buri site's structures). Graph: `takeoff` -> `atLeast {3, [protect group x5]}` through the strike window -> `land` at Buri; Bayug only when Buri's runway is unserviceable (F10-0's `land.at` set with a field-state condition). Secondary: save a damaged Ki-21 until it crosses the Buri threshold (`arrive` plus `protect`). Population: four friendly fighters; four F6F and two TBM attackers (10 at peak); AAA and parked aircraft are site actors, not extra active aircraft.

**J2 Break the Landing** (B5N2, 1515 broken cloud). The player and two B5Ns start southeast of Leyte Gulf with no friendly Zero or Val formation. Defenders: three F6F. Shipping: four Marus, two Fletchers, one Cleveland, one Pennsylvania, four LCVPs. Graph: `reach` (penetrate the CAP) -> `atLeast {1, [destroy a Maru, damage a Fletcher or the Cleveland below 0.5]}` -> `reach` the western or southern egress gate (`recovery: egress`; Decision 9). Secondary: destroy two LCVPs. Peak six aircraft, eight large ships, four landing craft. **A landing at an allied field is not a success here.**

**J3 Road to Burauen** (Ki-43-IIb, 0945 partly cloudy). The player and one Ki-43 orbit a moving column: four Type 97 (tag `tank`), eight Type 94, one Type 95. Four P-38s attack in two waves (held groups on timers). Graph: `arrive {column vehicles, count: 8, Buri dispersal zone}` with a second `arrive {tank tag, count: 2, same zone}` -> `land` at Bayug (legal in the J3 window as ruled). Secondary: the command car and all four tanks survive (`protect`, `maxLost: 0`). At most six airborne and thirteen powered ground vehicles, plus two destination AAA.
