## The new condition kind

One shape, `PlaneState`, every field optional, all given fields must hold at once, at least one required:

| Field | Meaning | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `gear` | `up` (fully retracted) or `down` (fully extended) | `state.gearFraction` |
| `flaps` | `up` or `down` | `state.flapFraction` |
| `bayDoors` | `shut` or `open` | `state.bayDoorFraction` |
| `airspeedMps` | `[min, max]`, air-relative | `length(airVelocity(state, wind))` |
| `altitudeM` | `[min, max]`, above sea level | `state.position.y` |
| `throttle` | `[min, max]`, 0 to 1, the lever | `controls.throttle` |

It appears twice: as an objective (`kind: "state"`, complete the first tick it holds, only while the player is alive) and as a trigger (`when: { state: {...} }`, fires once). SI in content, like every other key; the radio text says feet and miles per hour.

## Rulings (conservative defaults, Mark is away)

- **R1. Flaps are `up` or `down`.** The airplane has no intermediate setting, so "flaps 20 degrees" cannot be asked.
- **R2. No Assist condition.** See the table above. `U` is taught in text and the bomb run is secondary, so a player who never presses it loses nothing.
- **R3. The bomb run is secondary.** A player who picked the clean loadout cannot bomb; the tutorial must stay finishable. Briefing recommends `bombs`.
- **R4. Radio lines are text-only** (`null` in `RADIO_LINES`). The code has the sanctioned way; no recording is required.
- **R5. Order.** Gunnery and the bomb run come before the landing, not after: a landing ends the sortie, and landing twice would need a respot at an airfield.
- **R6. Instant, not held.** A state objective completes the first tick it holds. A hold-for-N-seconds variant is not needed by any step (YAGNI).
- **R7. Picker.** Placed before Carrier Qualification, so it is the first mission row.
- **R8. Name and badge:** "Basic Flying" (badge `basic-flying`, "Basic Flying"), a training sortie at Tacloban in late 1944.

## Steps

Each is a chained objective (`after` the one before); a radio message fires as each one starts.

| # | Objective | Kind | Wrong behavior that must keep it incomplete |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Full throttle | `state` throttle 0.9 to 1 | Throttle at idle |
| 2 | Take off | `takeoff` | Sitting on the runway |
| 3 | Gear up | `state` gear up | Gear left down |
| 4 | Climb to 1,500 ft | `state` altitude 1,500 to 5,000 ft | Staying low; flying above the band |
| 5 | Fly to the marker | `reach` over the bay (B1 arrow) | Flying elsewhere |
| 6 | Strafe the parked Hellcats | `destroy` | Not hitting them |
| 7 | Bomb the cargo ship (secondary) | `destroy` | Not bombing it; never gates the badge |
| 8 | Join the final | `reach` three miles south of the strip | |
| 9 | Gear and flaps down, under 120 mph | `state` gear down, flaps down, speed 0 to 120 mph | Any one of the three missing |
| 10 | Land | `land` at Tacloban | |

## Tasks

Sim first.

1. **Schema:** `PlaneStateObject`, the `state` objective kind and the `state` trigger `when` in `schema.ts`; cross-checks in `checkMission` (an empty `state` is rejected).
2. **Evaluation:** `planeStateHolds` in `step.ts`, used by `evaluate` and `conditionMet`; `wind` added to `MissionTick` and passed by `loop.ts`.
3. **Unit tests** (`tests/sim/mission/planeState.test.ts`): schema accepts and rejects; each field passes and fails in a real `advance`; fields combine with AND; a dead player completes nothing; as a trigger it fires once; identical worlds stay identical (determinism).
4. **Scenario** `content/scenarios/tutorial.json`: Tacloban start, parked targets, an anchored cargo ship, ten chained objectives, the messages, badge, briefing, history.
5. **Radio:** every tutorial message into `RADIO_LINES` as `null`.
6. **Picker row** in `SCENARIO_OPTIONS`; update the pinned mission list in `content.test.ts` and the e2e id union.
7. **Scripted pilot** (`tests/sim/mission/missions/tutorial.test.ts`): flies steps 1 to 5 and 8 to 10 through production `advance`, steps 6 and 7 by injected hits; asserts every objective in order and the badge.
8. **Wrong-behavior tests** in the same file: for each state step, the world where the player does the wrong thing keeps the objective active.
9. **Docs:** `GAMEPLAY.md` (the mission and the condition kind), `docs/testing.md` pointer if needed, `CONTEXT.md` ("Plane state"), and only the B4 bullet of `MASTER_PLAN.md`.
10. **GPU capture spec** (`tests/e2e/tutorialCapture.spec.ts`, skipped unless `E2E_CAPTURE=1`): launch the mission, drive the first steps with keys, read the objective through `__ww2`, capture the radio line and steering cue.
11. **Verify and hand off:** `remote-run npm run verify` green; captures on the ryzen console-session Playwright server; handoff `docs/handoff/2026-10-10-b4-tutorial.md`, mailed with the captures inline.

- New recorded voice lines.
