## Parts

- **B, sourcing.** The dossier, copied here. Stall, roll rate, g limit, gear and flap travel times are ESTIMATE.
- **C, sweep.** cd0 0.0175 and propulsive efficiency 0.68 fitted to the two sourced speeds; the fleet's lift curve is not fitted.
- **D, cards.** A `CARDS` entry with tolerances that state the misses; a green card is not validation where inputs are estimates.
- **E, model and register.** Committed glb and rig unchanged; stance, center point (station 0.3 default), eye point (`kabina-FACES`).
- **F, fit the drawing.** Gear measured from the model; mounts measured; guns and zones checked by `combatFit.test.ts`.
- **G, Tier 2.** `flyableAll.spec.ts -g ki-43`, `hangar.spec.ts`, `sortie.spec.ts`.
- **H, flyable in the game.** Selectable in the sortie UI in Dev, parks, takes off, drops a bomb.
