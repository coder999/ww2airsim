mounts slicer reads the propellers and booms as wing inboard of that.

## Parts

- **B, sourcing.** The dossier, with the J-table versus L variant mix. Roll rate, take-off
  distance, gear and flap travel times and lift-off speed are ESTIMATE.
- **C, sweep.** cd0 0.026 and propulsive efficiency 0.725 fitted to the speed and climb tables
  up to 20,000 ft. The fleet's shared lift curve (clMax 1.4) is not fitted; the stall miss is reported.
- **D, cards.** A `CARDS` entry with tolerances that state the misses; a green card is not
  validation where the inputs are estimates.
- **E, model and register.** Committed glb and rig unchanged; stance, center point, eye point.
- **F, fit the drawing.** Tricycle gear measured from the model; mounts measured.
- **G, Tier 2.** `flyableAll.spec.ts -g p-38`, `hangar.spec.ts`, `sortie.spec.ts`.
- **H, flyable in the game.** Selectable in the sortie UI, parks, takes off, drops a bomb.
