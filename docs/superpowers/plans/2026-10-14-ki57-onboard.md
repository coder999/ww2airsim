# Ki-57-II Topsy onboarding

The eighteenth airframe run through [`docs/aircraft.md`](../../aircraft.md): a Japanese Army twin-engine transport with
retractable main gear and a tailwheel. The original Blender model came from F4 (`docs/handoff/2026-10-14-f4-asset-groups.md`) as a
Hangar-only campaign asset; this run makes it flyable. Results, misses and open items:
[`docs/handoff/2026-10-14-ki57-onboard.md`](../../handoff/2026-10-14-ki57-onboard.md).

**Run shape (Mark, 2026-10-14):** worktree `~/projects/ww2airsim-worktrees/ki57-onboard`, branch `worktree-ki57-onboard`;
unattended, final product only. Never merge into `main`, push `main` or deploy. Mark delegated D1 to D6, D9 and D12 to D14 to the
recommended answers and chose D7/D8 and D10 himself.

**Added, not asked:** the audio family `multi_heavy`, and the Range Test scenarios regenerated to include the Topsy. A `bayDoors` block was tried and removed: `combat.test.ts` requires every spec with doors to drop bombs, and this airplane carries none.

**Deferred:** passengers and the paratrooper drop, engine-out, a modeled cockpit, any AI use, scenario placement (J5, J6, A6).

## Parts

- **B, sourcing.** TAIC TOPSY 1 sheet read from the page image (PDF p.209); the printed 2/8 mph digit is fixed by the printed 241 kt.
- **C, sweep.** cd0 0.017 and propulsive efficiency 0.65 fitted to four TAIC figures.
- **D, cards.** A `CARDS` entry with tolerances that state the misses.
- **E, model and register.** Committed glb and rig unchanged; `MODEL_STANCE`, eye-point canopy node, cockpit family, audio family.
- **F, fit the drawing.** Gear measured from the model.
- **G, Tier 2.** `flyableAll.spec.ts -g ki-57`, `hangar.spec.ts`.
- **H, flyable in the game.** Selectable in the sortie UI in Dev, parks, takes off.
