## New in this batch

- **AI airdrop attack:** `ingress.attack: 'airdrop'` on a pilot (scenario schema, `ingress` orders; AI controller): the transport flies its route, opens its doors in the corridor and drops its sticks inside the zone, then closes and egresses.
- **Player drop action:** a key bound to a drop, recorded in the flight record as a `drop` action with the world tick, applied at the same fixed tick on replay (the pattern of `requestBailout` and the lights switch). Format version bump and the verifier's application, with a replay test.
- **Effective drop:** a drop counts when the stick lands inside the marked zone and the aircraft was within the corridor (speed, altitude, heading) when the doors opened. `drop {targets, count, zone}` and a bundle counter `bundles {zone, min}`.
- **J4's `release` validity:** `release {store: torpedo, target, withinM, window}`.
- **J4 scheduled illumination:** flares as `LightSpec`s with a start and an end that also open and close the attack window through a trigger (the sim reads the window; the renderer reads the lights).

## Chapter notes

**J4 Destroyers in the Bay** (B5N2, 2130 night, Chūi; `coordinated: false`: an A6M2 high cover and an Allied TBM flare-layer would otherwise trip the gate). The B5N2 starts west of Ormoc Bay as the flare opens a single attack window. Graph: `reach {outside the flare}` (an annulus station, F10-0) -> valid torpedo `release` inside the window -> `damage` either Fletcher -> `protect` the Type B Maru through the window -> `reach` the western egress gate (Decision 9). Secondary: sink the damaged Fletcher. A ditching near friendly ships gives a normal debrief, no badge. Peak three aircraft, five ships. No torpedosight (Chūi is below the rank gate).

**J5 Night Drop on Burauen** (Ki-57-II, 1830, Taii). The player leads two Ki-57s from west of the ridge with a Ki-43 and an A6M2 escort; four P-38s oppose. Graph: hold the corridor (the F10-0 dwell with heading, speed and altitude, failing on exit) -> `drop {targets: player and at least one AI Ki-57, count: 2, zone}` -> `reach` the western egress gate. Secondary: all three transports drop and at least four of six bundles land in the marked area. Population: nine aircraft; six squad rigs plus two bundles per effective transport (18 rigs and 6 bundles at most). **State transition:** the existing J5 success creates the Japanese lodgment J6 uses; no field is captured here.
