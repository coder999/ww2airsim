### `disengageThreshold`: the arithmetic that decides it

7b's scorer already carries an energy disengage. With no threat astern and the angle and range penalties at zero, Pursue beats Extend when `E (2 - d) + 1000 d >= 0`, `E` the relative energy in J/kg and `d` the skill's `energyDiscipline`: the veteran (0.7) holds Pursue down to E = -538 J/kg, the green (0.3) to -176. The presets' thresholds (-400, -150) sit **inside** those crossovers, so a forced Extend at the threshold, as 7b's design wrote it, would make both presets quit earlier than they do now: the veteran most of all, the opposite of "holds the fight longer". Forcing it would also override a Break, a turn into an attacker that is the right answer with the energy gone. So the field is deleted, not wired; the energy disengage it described is `energyDiscipline`'s. This is a ruling for Mark (handoff).

## Wingman commands: design

- **Keys:** `1` Attack, `2` Cover me, `3` Form up. Free in flight: the digit keys are bound only in the replay's own key map, which owns the keyboard while a replay is up. Edge-triggered.
- **Who obeys:** every live AI whose `pilot.formation.leader` is the player. No wingman, no call.
- **State:** an optional `order` on the wingman's `FormationOrders`; absent is today's 7f behavior, so every shipped mission flies as before until the player gives an order. Set by a pure `orderWingmen(world, order)` in `src/sim/ai/wingmanOrders.ts`, called by `nextFrameState` on the key edge; it forces a rescore that tick.
- **Attack:** the target is the autopilot's while Shift holds one, else the nearest live enemy within 60° of the player's nose, else none. The wingman fights that target, as a static target, until it is down; then the order lapses to today's behavior. With no target, the wingman is cleared to engage anything it detects.
- **Cover me:** the wingman holds trail cover (`TRAIL_COVER`, 7f) and engages only what attacks the player or itself: a contact with the player or the wingman in its gun cone, one whose pilot is targeting the player, or a recent hitter of either.
- **Form up:** the wingman drops its target and keeps its finger-four station; it fights only in self-defense (a contact with it in its gun cone, or a recent hitter).
- **Radio:** a text-only line on the HUD radio, merged by tick like the friendly-fire call: "Wingman: Roger, engaging." / "Wingman: Roger, I'm on your tail." / "Wingman: Roger, rejoining."
- **Legend:** three rows.
