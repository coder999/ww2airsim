- **E, model and register.** Committed glb rebuilt at the shifted origin; rig unchanged; stance, center point, eye point.

- **G, Tier 2.** `flyableAll.spec.ts -g ki-84`, `hangar.spec.ts`, `sortie.spec.ts`.
- **H, flyable in the game.** Selectable in the sortie UI in Dev, parks, takes off, drops a bomb.
