## Parts

- **B, sourcing.** The PFOI's charts (weight 110,000 lb) for speed, climb, stall and
  ground run; Wikipedia for history, wing area and the 20,000 lb load. No flown test
  report exists.
- **C, sweep.** cd0 0.018 and propEfficiency 0.65 fitted to ten figures; the 7,620 m
  power fraction 0.87 is a compromise between a speed chart and a climb chart.
- **D, CARDS entry.** Measured errors in its comment; take-off is graded at a 32% band
  and that gap is reported.
- **E, register.** Stance (tricycle, level), center point (station 0.1, minX -6,
  tolerance 0.20), eye point (`b29_dark`), Library card with `spec`.
- **F, fit the drawing.** Gear read from the drawn model; `stance.test.ts` and
  `gearContact.test.ts` generalized for a tricycle; internal bay test extended.
- **G and H.** See the handoff.
