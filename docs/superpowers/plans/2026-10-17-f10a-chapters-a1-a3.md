## Chapter notes

**A1 Over the Beaches** (F6F-5, 0800 partly cloudy, `coordinated: false` is not needed: Ensign with a wingman pair is one type). Player and two F6Fs launch from Essex 12-15 mi east of the landing area (the existing fit row places Essex 13.5 mi seaward on the beach frame's axis; keep unless the placement pass finds a problem). Four Type B Marus, one Fletcher, one Cleveland (visual naval fire at inland points only), four LCVPs on shuttle routes between the Marus and the beach.
Badge graph in the vocabulary: `takeoff` -> raid contact (`reach` or `arrive` on the raid group) -> `protect {4 marus, maxLost: 1}` with a window that closes when the raid group is gone (`groupGone`) -> `land` (any friendly base; Essex is the suggested point). Secondary: `destroy {bombers, count: 2}` inside a window that closes when a bomber enters release range. Population: friendly 3 airborne; enemy four G4M, four A6M2, two D3A (13 at peak). Held groups: the raid in two waves so the peak holds. State transition: existing.

**A2 Taffy 3** (TBM-3, 0730 broken cloud and squalls, `coordinated: false`: F3 stages two F4F-4 stand-ins, which would otherwise trip the Commander gate). The player and two TBMs start airborne about 8 mi southeast of the engagement, the distance that meets F3's 2-3 min (the current invented point gives 189 s). Friendly: Casablanca, two Fletchers, two F4F-4 CAP (FM-2 stand-ins, disclosed). Enemy: Yamato, Mogami, Kagero, Shiratsuyu. Peak five aircraft and seven ships.
Graph: `reach` (join the attack) -> `damage {yamato, mogami (anyOf), belowHpFraction}` -> `protect {casablanca}` across the attack window -> `land` (Casablanca trap, or any friendly base). Secondary: `damage` on one Japanese destroyer ("disable", a threshold below sinking). **Placement of the threshold** is a measured figure: pick it from the weapon margin test (what one torpedo, or one bomb load, does to each hull's `hullHp`) so the primary needs a real hit but not a sinking; record the figure and the margin. The TBM torpedo arms on water entry; Ensign has no torpedosight (GAMEPLAY), so the player aims by eye.
**State transition:** a new `SUCCESS` row writes Tacloban and Dulag `buildup` (Decision 10) with the `worldRoundTrip` and `worldStates` tests. F3's "no transition on A2" line is amended in the F10A handoff and the spec.

**A3 The Long Column** (P-38L, 0930 partly cloudy). Player and one P-38 wingman start west of the ridge. Column: six Type 97 (tag `tank`), eight Type 94 cargo trucks, one Type 95 command car, two Type 38 field guns (stationary, not powered), two roadside AAA. Fifteen powered vehicles move on road waypoints; the nineteen entities are the spec's representative count.
Graph: `destroy {powered tag, count: 10}` and `destroy {tank tag, count: 2}` as two primaries (the `including two tanks` composition) -> `land` at Tacloban (`land.at` a set of one; legal because of A2's transition). Secondary: command car and both field guns. **The column is the first moving ground target set:** C4 measures its speed, spacing and road geometry so the player cannot kill ten before the contact window opens (the "no auto-complete" window), and checks the AAA does not trivialize the pass. Two AAA emplacements count as scenery targets with no objective weight.
