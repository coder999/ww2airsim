## Goal

Ship the four F3-locked airborne models: `ki-57-ii-topsy`, `parachuting-person-rig`, `paratrooper-supply-bundle`, and `m24-aircraft-parachute-flare`. ## Ownership and coordination

This branch owns the small shared `equipment` Library/model kind needed by non-building, non-vehicle display assets. Maritime and night/site may author their Blender scripts in parallel, but they rebase on this foundation before adding their equipment registrations. The branch does not add a Ki-57 flight specification or campaign behavior; the aircraft is Hangar-only until later F7/F10 integration.

Primary Blender execution uses a Ryzen remote-run slot. Nexus may perform the final deterministic rebuild if the remote runner cannot return generated bytes safely. Existing model outputs must remain byte-identical.
