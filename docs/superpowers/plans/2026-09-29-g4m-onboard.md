## Parts

- **B, sourcing.** Figures cite EN-WP, JA-WP, PWE and TAIC-1 with read dates. The
  Betty 22 page in TAIC-1 is a different airplane and is not used.
- **C, sweep.** cd0 0.020 and propEfficiency 0.56 fitted to the single top-speed
  point and the climb figure; power curve DERIVED from the Kasei 11 military rating;
  `flap.clIncrement` 0.3565 derived from an ESTIMATED clean stall and a weakly
  sourced flap stall, so it is circular (disclosed).
- **D, CARDS entry.** In `graded.test.ts` with the measured errors in its comment;
  take-off runs the direction check only.
- **E, rig and register.** Stance, center point (station 0.2, minX -6), eye point,
  Library card with `spec`. The rig rows already existed.
- **F, fit the drawing.** Gear geometry read from the drawn model; internal bay test
  extended to the G4M.
- **G, Tier 2.** See the handoff.
