## New in this batch

- **`near {entity, radiusM}`** as a plane-state field: the distance from the player to a named entity.
- **`landedNear {entity | point, radiusM}`**: a `land` variant that checks the landing report's position (A4: a supported water landing within 150 yd of the raft).
- **`pickup {raft, survivors, withinFt, groundSpeedKt, dwellS}`**: complete when the player stays within the radius below the speed for the dwell, which moves `survivors` aboard (state on the raft entity; the entities are recorded sim state, so the flight record stays deterministic). It composes F10-0's dwell, ground speed and `near`, so it is a thin objective, not new motion code.
- **Rescue entities:** a raft group (three survivors), a separated survivor with a dye marker, and the scripted 63-ft launch. They are placed in the scenario like any floating vehicle; boarding state lives on the entity.

## Chapter notes

**A4 Dumbo in the Gulf** (PBY-5A, 2100 night, scattered cloud, placed on 5 November). The PBY starts afloat at the lit San Pedro Bay anchorage (the Jinamoc site, buildup window). Graph: `takeoff` (water) -> `reach` the 10 mi search area -> acquire the survivor flare (a scheduled light visible inside a range; a `reach`/`near` check) -> `landedNear {raft, 150 yd}` -> `pickup {raft, 3 survivors, 100 ft, 2 kt, 20 s}` -> `land` at the anchorage with ground speed below 1 kt and engines idle for 10 s (dwell, throttle at idle). Secondary: recover the separated survivor (a second `pickup` after the first). After pickup, two Ki-43s make one recon pass; the group spawns when the first `pickup` completes (`completed` trigger) and they do not spawn earlier. Peak air population three. The briefing must say no specific documented rescue is reenacted.
The placement pass labels the raft point, the search area centre and the flare schedule with the F3 limits they meet (search area inside 10 mi; first action 5-6 min including the water takeoff). The 63-ft launch route is dressing with a `landing` role for the second survivor; its waypoints are checked over water.

**A5 Close the Ormoc Lifeline** (F6F-5, 1030 broken cloud, Commander, so `coordinated` applies as written). The player and one F6F start with four TBMs westbound toward Ormoc. Graph: `hold` an escort station that follows the TBM group (`hold relativeTo`, built in F10-0) -> `released {tbm, torpedo or bomb, count: 3}` -> `arrive {tbm, count: 3, rally gate}` with `protect` through the window -> `land` (Essex suggested; any friendly base legal). Secondary: destroy one escort after the release event (`destroy` with `window.from` the release objective). Population: friendly player, one F6F, four TBMs; enemy four A6M2, two Ki-43; ships Essex, three Marus, Kagero, Shiratsuyu; peak 12 aircraft and six ships.
The player is an escort, not the striker: C4 measures that the TBMs survive when escorted and are lost unescorted at each difficulty (the escort-mission pattern), and that the convoy does not die without the TBMs' release.
