## The original decision (not Mark's): doors drawn in code

The recorded line said the doors would reuse C1 batch 1's hinge machinery: pieces in the Blender scripts, pivoted from the sidecar. This plan draws them in code instead (`src/render/scene/bayDoors.ts`) for all four bombers:

- **The B-17 is a download.** Its raw source is not on nexus (`tools/models/cache/boeing_b-17_flying_fortress.glb` is absent, verified 2026-10-08).
- **A rebuild would re-simplify its body.** `split` runs before `simplify`, so any new split changes every body triangle.
- **One path, not two.** Code for the Sketchfab model and kit work for the Blender three would be two paths. Code serves all four, rebuilds no glb, and stays out of the C1 batch 2 fork's way (it owns the manifest's vector pivot axis).
- **The cost:** flat plates on a curved belly, each offset 3.5 cm outward so the skin's sag never shows through. Painted a flat per-airframe color, over a dark well plate that reads as the open bay.

Each bay's keel and hinge heights are ray-cast off the committed glb. `aircraftRigs.test.ts` holds them within 3 cm.

| Airframe | Bays (body x, m) | Half width | Keel y | Hinge y | Travel | Drag area |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B-29 | 2.0 to 5.0, -7.0 to -4.0 | 0.6 | -1.449 | -1.318 | 2 s | 1.0 m² |
| B-17 | -1.5 to 1.5 | 0.4 | -0.904 | -0.838 | 10 s | 0.45 m² |
| G4M | -2.6 to 1.2 | 0.5 | -1.262 | -1.164 | 6 s | 0.36 m² |
| Ki-21 | -2.5 to 1.3 | 0.4 | -1.003 | -0.880 | 6 s | 0.3 m² |

The bays span each spec's `bay-*` racks plus about a bomb's length. Travel and drag are ESTIMATEs, labeled in each spec's `bayDoors.source`.

## Tasks

1. **Sim** (`src/sim/bayDoors.ts`):
   - `spec.bayDoors`, `Controls.bayDoorsOpen` and `AircraftState.bayDoorFraction`;
   - `bayDoorsAfter`, the flaps' twin;
   - `bayDoorDragN` in `step`'s drag sum;
   - `bayDoorsShut` gates `releaseBomb`.
2. **AI stub:**
   - `ingressBayDoorsOpen`: within `BAY_DOORS_OPEN_RANGE_M` (5,000 m, an ESTIMATE) of the ingress destination, with it ahead;
   - applied once, in `pilotTick`, for any raider whose spec has doors.
3. **Frame and HUD:**
   - the `O` lever (`toggleBayDoors`), edge-triggered like the hook;
   - `bayDoorsNoticeS`: 2.5 s of "BAY DOORS CLOSED" in the autopilot badge's row after a refused `V`;
   - a BAY row in the flight-data strip;
   - the control legend.
4. **Renderer:**
   - `bayDoors.ts`, from `AirframeRig.bays`;
   - `AirframeUpdate.bayDoorFraction`;
   - a `doors` bench part, with a slider and Cycle (the real `bayDoorsAfter`).
5. **Sound:**
   - `synth.ts` renders the motor, a 110 Hz band-limited sawtooth with flutter, as a one-second seamless loop at load;
   - the motor is a `LAYERS` row like any recording, through a new `loadSamples` backend verb;
   - `nextAudio` runs it while the doors travel, held two ticks so a frame that ran no fixed step does not flicker it, and clunks at each end.
6. **Tests:** enroll, don't clone.
   - The combat gate over all four bay bombers, pinned to the specs with `bayDoors`.
   - The door rigs pinned to the same list, and the belly stance.
   - Sim travel, drag and the gate's predicate.
   - Drag through the real step.
   - The AI stub through production `advance`.
   - The frame lever and notice; the audio motor, clunk and loop seam; the flight-data row.
   - The door motion; Hangar E2E 7b (Cycle opens every bomber's doors, visibly) and 13 (gizmos).
